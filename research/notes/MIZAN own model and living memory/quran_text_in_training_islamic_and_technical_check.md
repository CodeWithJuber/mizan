# The Quran as training data: resolving Ruh's 30%-Quran pipeline against the "never a training target" rule, with an Islamic-scholarship check and a measured garbling canary

Researcher note for the MIZAN own-model project, 30 October 2026 (treating 29 October 2026 as "today" per the owner's constraints). Builds on, and does not repeat, `audit_ruh_model_state.md` §2 (data mix, `training_permission`, Tanzil/KFGQPC provenance), `quranic_model_of_teaching_learning_memory.md` KQ1 §C–E and KQ4 #14, `tiny_language_model_on_cpu.md` KQ6, and the 28 October report's "Islamic corpora are sources, not seeds" and "scripture gets no novelty" (the licence side is settled there: Tanzil CC BY 3.0 verbatim-only, QAC GPL + non-commercial FAQ, Quran Foundation terms banning ML training). Labels: **DIRECT** (the text says it), **TAFSIR** (a classical commentary says it), **ISHARA** (an analogy this researcher draws). Numbers are tagged [measured] / [estimated] / [inference]. Quran text is Saheeh International from quran.com (`?translations=20`); Arabic tafsir renderings are marked "(model tr.)"; hadith come from the fawazahmed0 hadith-api editions with the graders' verdicts; the Quran text used in the experiment is the pinned `risan/quran-json` Tanzil Uthmani copy the Ruh pipeline itself downloads (SHA-256 `adaebb37…b761`, verified [measured]).

Headline: the Islamic sources give no ruling on *training* a statistical model on the mushaf (no fatwa body has addressed it, a gap recorded below), but they give a very clear ruling on the *output side*: a string that is nearly but not exactly the Quran, presented as the Quran, is the textbook case of lahn jali / tahrif, and faulty copies are to be corrected or destroyed, never circulated. On the memorisation literature a byte model trained with the Quran as a next-token target is *expected* to produce such strings at scale; the measured canary (KQ3, 1.9M parameters) found no copies and no near-copies at that size but found something the rulings also cover: 99 % of the target-trained model's continuations were invented words written in Uthmani mushaf script (0 % for the no-Quran control). The offline check of MIZAN's current quote-fidelity gate showed that it lets single-word and diacritic-only alterations through. The consistent resolution is: the Quran may be an **input** (to analysers, retrievers and lookup tables) but never a **target** (next-byte loss), and "Quranic root-space" must be read as "a root inventory catalogued from Quranic Arabic morphology", not "a model that has learned the Quran".

## Key question 1 — What the Quran, authentic Sunnah and recognised scholarship say that bears, directly or by analogy, on a machine model trained on the Quran's text or producing Quran-like text

### Takeaway
No verse, hadith or fatwa addresses statistical training as such; what is DIRECT is the prohibition on producing text "so you may think it is from the Scripture, but it is not" (3:78), on writing text and attributing it to Allah (2:79), on changing words from their places (2:75, 4:46, 5:13, 7:162), on claiming to produce "the like of" revelation (2:23, 17:88, 6:93), and on saying of Allah what one does not know (16:116, 17:36). The fiqh of recitation and of the mushaf applies the same standard to a single wrong word (lahn jali: haram for one who can avoid it) and to a faulty printed copy (correct it if possible, otherwise bury or burn; never circulate). Contemporary bodies (Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya, January 2026; Al-Azhar's Observatory, September 2024; IslamWeb, March 2025; IslamQA, September 2024; the OIC Fiqh Academy, May 2025) permit AI *display* and *recitation aids* only under verification by the authorities who verify the mushaf, forbid taking Quranic *meanings* from AI independently, and say nothing about training; KFGQPC, the Saudi senior-scholar bodies, Jordan's Iftaa', Tarteel and the Quran Foundation have published no statement on AI generation of Quranic text that this session could find.

### Cited Findings

**A. Preservation (15:9, 85:21–22, 75:16–17) — what "the Quran" is**
- 15:9: "Indeed, it is We who sent down the message [i.e., the Qur'ān], and indeed, We will be its guardian." — [Quran 15:9](https://quran.com/15:9?translations=20). Ibn Kathir (English abridgement): Allah "is the One Who revealed the Dhikr to him, which is the Qur'an, and He is protecting it from being changed or altered." — [Ibn Kathir on 15:6–9](https://quran.com/15:6/tafsirs/169). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): "He preserved its words from change, addition and omission, and its meanings from substitution; no distorter distorts one of its meanings except that Allah raises up someone who makes the clear truth plain" (الفاظه من التغيير فيها والزيادة والنقص، ومعانيه من التبديل) — [al-Sa'di on 15:9](https://quran.com/15:9/tafsirs/91). Strength: preservation is DIRECT; that it covers *words* against change, addition and omission is TAFSIR (al-Sa'di). Mapping (ISHARA): the only thing that may be called "the Quran" in MIZAN is the transmitted text in a pinned edition; a model's weights never participate in preservation, and a generated string is not "the Quran" however close it is.
- 85:21–22: "But this is an honored Qur'ān / [Inscribed] in a Preserved Slate." — [Quran 85:21–22](https://quran.com/85:21-22?translations=20). 75:16–17: "Move not your tongue with it, [O Muḥammad], to hasten with it [i.e., recitation of the Qur'ān]. / Indeed, upon Us is its collection [in your heart] and [to make possible] its recitation." — [Quran 75:16–17](https://quran.com/75:16-17?translations=20). Strength: DIRECT that the text's collection and recitation are Allah's doing, received, not composed. Mapping (ISHARA): reception by lookup (Fihris), not composition by a generator.

**B. Producing scripture-like text and attributing it (2:79, 3:78, 6:93, 2:23, 17:88)**
- 2:79: "So woe to those who write the 'scripture' with their own hands, then say, 'This is from Allāh,' in order to exchange it for a small price. Woe to them for what their hands have written and woe to them for what they earn." — [Quran 2:79](https://quran.com/2:79?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: Ibn Abbas (in al-Bukhari): "the People of the Book altered the Book of Allah, changed it and wrote another book with their own hands. They then said, 'This book is from Allah'"; the woe is "because of what they have written with their own hands, the lies, falsehood and alterations." — [Ibn Kathir on 2:78–79](https://quran.com/2:78/tafsirs/169). Al-Sa'di (model tr.) quotes Ibn Taymiyya that the verse "reaches whoever writes with his hand a book contrary to the Book of Allah, to gain the world by it, and says it is from Allah" — [al-Sa'di on 2:79](https://quran.com/2:79/tafsirs/91). Strength: DIRECT condemnation of man-made text passed off as revelation; the extension to anyone who writes contrary text and attributes it is TAFSIR (Ibn Taymiyya via al-Sa'di). Mapping (ISHARA): a machine has no intention, but the *system* that prints a generated near-ayah under "Allah says (2:255)" performs the attribution the verse condemns; the burden falls on MIZAN's presentation layer, which is why Sidq's replace-by-source rule exists.
- 3:78: "And indeed, there is among them a party who alter the Scripture with their tongues so you may think it is from the Scripture, but it is not from the Scripture. And they say, 'This is from Allāh,' but it is not from Allāh. And they speak untruth about Allāh while they know." — [Quran 3:78](https://quran.com/3:78?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: they "distort Allah's Words with their tongues, change them from their appropriate places, and alter their intended meanings … to deceive the ignorant people by making it appear that their words are in the Book of Allah"; Wahb ibn Munabbih: "the people misguide others by addition and false interpretation, relying on books that they wrote themselves" while the revealed books themselves remain preserved — [Ibn Kathir on 3:78](https://quran.com/3:78/tafsirs/169). Strength: DIRECT. Mapping (ISHARA, strong): "so you may think it is from the Scripture, but it is not" is the precise description of a near-Quranic garbled string; the verse's own test is *resemblance that deceives*, which is what the canary measures (edit distance 0 < d ≤ 0.2).
- 6:93: "And who is more unjust than one who invents a lie about Allāh or says, 'It has been inspired to me,' while nothing has been inspired to him, and one who says, 'I will reveal [something] like what Allāh revealed.'" — [Quran 6:93](https://quran.com/6:93?translations=20). 2:23: "And if you are in doubt about what We have sent down … then produce a sūrah the like thereof" — [Quran 2:23](https://quran.com/2:23?translations=20). 17:88: "If mankind and the jinn gathered in order to produce the like of this Qur'ān, they could not produce the like of it" — [Quran 17:88](https://quran.com/17:88?translations=20). Strength: DIRECT that producing "the like of" the Quran is both impossible and, when claimed, a grave lie. Mapping (ISHARA, to be stated carefully): a generator whose objective is literally "predict the Quran's next byte" is being *optimised to produce the like of it*; the verses do not speak of statistics, but they fix the attitude MIZAN must take toward such an objective — no such capability is to be built or advertised, and a sampled continuation is never presented as a verse.

**C. Changing words from their places (tahrif) — 2:75, 4:46, 5:13, 7:162**
- 2:75: "… a party of them used to hear the words of Allāh and then distort it [i.e., the Torah] after they had understood it while they were knowing" — [Quran 2:75](https://quran.com/2:75?translations=20). 4:46: "those who distort words from their [proper] places [i.e., usages] … twisting their tongues" — [Quran 4:46](https://quran.com/4:46?translations=20). 5:13: "They distort words from their [proper] places … and have forgotten a portion of that of which they were reminded" — [Quran 5:13](https://quran.com/5:13?translations=20). 7:162: "those who wronged among them changed [the words] to a statement other than that which had been said to them" — [Quran 7:162](https://quran.com/7:162?translations=20). Strength: DIRECT condemnation of altering transmitted words. Mapping (ISHARA): a model that substitutes one word of an ayah (the canary's commonest failure) is doing mechanically what 7:162 describes; 5:13 adds *omission* ("forgotten a portion") to *substitution*.

**D. Speaking of Allah without knowledge — 16:116, 22:30, 17:36**
- 16:116: "And do not say about what your tongues assert of untruth, 'This is lawful and this is unlawful,' to invent falsehood about Allāh" — [Quran 16:116](https://quran.com/16:116?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: "This includes everyone who comes up with an innovation for which he has no evidence from the Shari`ah" — [Ibn Kathir on 16:114–117](https://quran.com/16:114/tafsirs/169). 22:30: "… and avoid false statement" — [Quran 22:30](https://quran.com/22:30?translations=20). 17:36: "And do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge. Indeed, the hearing, the sight and the heart - about all those [one] will be questioned." — [Quran 17:36](https://quran.com/17:36?translations=20). Strength: DIRECT. Mapping: these are the verses the Egyptian AI-tafsir fatwa (below) rests on; for MIZAN they are DIRECT support for "no tafsir, ruling or ayah from a model's own words".

**E. Hadith: fabrication, the pinned text and its authorised variants**
- Lying on the Prophet ﷺ: "Whoever tells a lie against me (intentionally), then (surely) let him occupy his seat in Hell-fire" — Sahih al-Bukhari 110 ([Bukhari 110](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/110.json)); also Bukhari 106 ('Ali), 107 (al-Zubayr), 109 (Salamah: "Whoever (intentionally) ascribes to me what I have not said"), 3461 ("Convey (my teachings) to the people even if it were a single sentence … whoever tells a lie on me intentionally, will surely take his place in the (Hell) Fire") — [Bukhari 106](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/106.json), [107](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/107.json), [109](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/109.json), [3461](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/3461.json). Strength: DIRECT (mutawatir in meaning). Mapping: a generated "hadith", or a garbled real one presented as a hadith, is ascribing to him what he did not say; the 28 October report's "a novel verse or hadith is fabricated by definition" stands.
- Bukhari 4987 (Uthman's copies and the burning of every other Quranic material) and 5041 (two authorised recitations of al-Furqan: "It was revealed to be recited in this way … so recite of it that which is easier for you") are quoted in full in `quranic_model_of_teaching_learning_memory.md` KQ1 §E ([Bukhari 4987](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/4987.json), [Bukhari 5041](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/5041.json)); re-fetched here and unchanged [measured]. Strength: DIRECT history. Mapping (ISHARA): the set of acceptable texts is closed (pinned editions and the listed readings); stray copies are destroyed, not kept "for training".
- A popular narration that must **not** be used: "Whoever says (something) about the Qur'an without knowledge, then let him take his seat in the Fire" (Tirmidhi 2950) and "whoever says (something) about the Qur'an according to his (own) opinion, then let him take his seat in the Fire" (Tirmidhi 2951) are graded **Da'if** by Ahmad Muhammad Shakir, al-Albani and Zubair Ali Zai in the edition; Tirmidhi 2952 and Abu Dawud 3652 ("If anyone interprets the Book of Allah in the light of his opinion even if he is right, he has erred") are Da'if per al-Albani, Abd al-Hamid, al-Arna'ut and Zubair Ali Zai (Shakir: "Sahih Isnaad Maqtu" for 2952 only) — [Tirmidhi 2950](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-tirmidhi/2950.json), [2951](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-tirmidhi/2951.json), [2952](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-tirmidhi/2952.json), [Abu Dawud 3652](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-abudawud/3652.json). The principle is carried by 16:116 and 17:36 (DIRECT), not by these reports.

**F. Lahn (error) in recitation and in the written mushaf**
- Definition and ruling: al-lahn al-jali is "an error in the wording that breaks the recognised manner of recitation, whether or not it changes the meaning", recognisable by non-specialists, "whether by swapping a letter or changing a vowel or sukun"; ruling: "يحرم على من هو قادر على تلافيه" (forbidden for one who is able to avoid it); al-lahn al-khafi (letter qualities only, no change of meaning) is not an individual obligation that incurs punishment — [IslamOnline fiqh, "الإخلال بأحكام التجويد", 9 September 2024](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ae%d9%84%d8%a7%d9%84-%d8%a8%d8%a3%d8%ad%d9%83%d8%a7%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ac%d9%88%d9%8a%d8%af/). Examples from *al-Mizan fi ahkam tajwid al-Qur'an* (Firyal Zakariya al-'Abd, Dar al-Iman, Cairo): replacing a word ("عليم حكيم" read as "عزيز حكيم"), adding a word, omitting a word ("مؤمنة" dropped from "تحرير رقبة"), adding or dropping an alif (لا أقسم → لأقسم), all listed under lahn jali — [usul.ai text of al-Mizan, section 1](https://usul.ai/t/the-scale-in-the-rules-of-quranic-recitation/27). Strength: DIRECT (fiqh of recitation) for the human reciter. Mapping (ISHARA): a model's single-word substitution is the textbook lahn jali example; the "able to avoid it" condition is always met by a system that can look the text up, so the obligation to avoid it is unconditional for MIZAN.
- Faulty copies: the Saudi Permanent Committee's fatwa no. 6252 (English translation): "it is obligatory to correct the errors to whatever extent possible"; if not feasible, dispose of the copy "by either burning or burying it"; no. 176: where writing or "printing and/or publishing errors" cannot be corrected "it is permitted to bury it without burning it", or burn and bury the remains away from walkways; no. 8381: burn or bury in a clean place; fatwa 176 cites al-Bukhari that Uthman "ordered that all other pages and copies" be burnt — [Salafy Ink translation of the Permanent Committee's verdicts, 19 June 2024](https://salafyink.substack.com/p/what-should-be-done-with-a-mushaf) (secondary translation; no volume/page given). IslamWeb (9 April 2025): unreadable pages may be buried or burned "to protect them from desecration", citing Uthman's burning in al-Bukhari — [IslamWeb 510508](https://www.islamweb.net/en/printfatwa.php?id=510508). Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya (fatwa 661, 15 May 2008): "the basic principle is that burning a mushaf from which one can still recite is prohibited"; the majority permit burning one "physically unfit to use for recitation" — [Dar al-Ifta 661](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/661/burning-pages-from-the-qur%E2%80%99an). Strength: DIRECT (contemporary fatwa, unanimous on the point that an erroneous copy is corrected or withdrawn). Mapping (ISHARA, strong): a model output that is a corrupted ayah is a faulty copy; the only two lawful fates are correction (replace with the source: Sidq) or destruction (never shown, never stored); keeping it "as data" has no precedent.

**G. Iqtibas (borrowing Quranic phrases in one's own speech): where altered wording is tolerated and where it is not**
- Definition (al-Mawsu'a al-Fiqhiyya 6/16–17, quoted by IslamQA): "تضمين المتكلم كلامه - شعراً كان أو نثراً - شيئاً من القرآن، أو الحديث" — the speaker embedding Quran or hadith in his own speech, verse or prose, *without signalling that it is Quran*; the encyclopaedia's first type is borrowing that keeps the meaning "with a slight change of wording" (تغيير يسير), its second moves the phrase to a new meaning — [IslamQA (Arabic) 119673, "الاقتباس من القرآن، أنواعه وأحكامه", 22 July 2008](https://islamqa.info/ar/119673). Al-Suyuti (al-Itqan, quoting Ibn Hijjah's Sharh al-Badi'iyyah) grades iqtibas as مقبول (accepted: sermons, exhortations, covenants), مباح (permitted: love poetry, letters, stories) and مردود (rejected, two kinds: attributing to oneself what Allah attributed to Himself, and quoting a verse in jest or obscenity), and says "وهذا التقسيم حسن جدّاً وبه أقول" — same source; the English answer cites al-Itqan 1/387 and al-Hawi li'l-Fatawa 1/305 for al-Suyuti's view that there is no disagreement about the permissibility of appropriate reference — [IslamQA 203026, 17 February 2014](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/203026). The Maliki school: "اشتهر عند المالكية تحريمه وتشديد النكير على فاعله", though some Malikis disliked it only in verse and allowed prose, and al-Qadi 'Iyad and Ibn Daqiq al-'Id used it in prose; Hanafi jurists used it in their books; al-'Izz ibn 'Abd al-Salam permitted it — [IslamOnline fiqh, "الاقتباس من القرآن بين الجواز والمنع", 22 July 2024](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B3-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A2%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B9/); the same article's condition: the borrowing "must not be presented as Quranic or hadith text". Ibn Taymiyya (Mukhtasar al-Fatawa al-Misriyya p. 172): the Quran's wording may not be used for other than what Allah revealed it for; mocking it is kufr — [IslamQA 203026](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/203026). Strength: TAFSIR/fiqh (a classical, cross-madhhab doctrine). Mapping: the doctrine *defines* the one case in which altered Quranic wording is lawful: when the words are the speaker's own speech and are **not presented as the Quran**. Everything MIZAN's model might emit that resembles an ayah must therefore either be (a) MIZAN's own prose, never labelled as Quran and never under a reference, or (b) a verbatim lookup. The "rejected" kind — attributing to oneself what Allah attributed to Himself — is DIRECT warning against a model speaking in the Quran's first-person divine voice.

**H. Contemporary bodies, with what each actually says (training / generating / displaying separated)**
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya, fatwa 24202, 18 January 2026 (Grand Mufti Nazir Muhammad Ayyad):** "It is permissible in Islamic law to use AI technology to create electronic copies of the Mushaf" and AI-generated recitations, "provided that such technology serves as a means of helping those who memorize the Quran maintain proper recitation", that "such computer programs must first be reviewed and approved by the authorities responsible for verifying the Mushaf", that "this modern technology must adhere to the rules of tajweed and recitation" (stricter for the qira'at), and that it not "infringe upon the intellectual property rights of others"; "means take the ruling of their ends". It does not separate display, generation and training — [Dar al-Ifta 24202](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/24202/the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-the-creation-of-electronic-mushafs-and-ai-g). Kind: a fatwa. Reading: *generating* (recitation) and *displaying* (e-mushaf) are permitted only under prior review by the mushaf-verifying authority; a stochastic generator cannot be "reviewed and approved" as a fixed text can, so it cannot satisfy the condition [inference].
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya, fatwa 8856, 6 January 2026 (same mufti):** question "ما حكم الاعتماد على تطبيقات الذكاء الاصطناعي كـ (شات جي بي تي) في تفسير القرآن الكريم؟"; verdict: total reliance is "ممنوعٌ شرعًا" and the Quran's meanings "لا تُتَلَقَّى" from these tools "استقلالًا"; reasons: "جهالةُ المصدر، وعدمُ إمكانِ التحقُّق من سلامةِ المنقول", the risk of tafsir by opinion, the absence of "منهجًا علميًّا محرَّرًا في التفسير", and the vendor's own admission that answers can be plausible but wrong; remedy: the recognised tafsir books, qualified scholars and accredited institutions — [Dar al-Ifta 8856 (Arabic)](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/22413/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A2%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A); English press summary (27 January 2026): "religiously impermissible" to rely solely on AI to interpret the Quran — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/can-muslims-use-ai-to-interpret-holy-quran-1.500422462). Kind: a fatwa. Reading: an AI's *interpretation* is forbidden as an independent source precisely because its source is unknown and unverifiable — the same two defects a next-token generator has for the *text* [inference].
- **Al-Azhar Observatory for Combating Extremism, statement of 21 September 2024:** on AI-made "Quranic songs" (verses set to AI-generated Western music): "it is forbidden by Sharia to read it accompanied by music in any form"; the trend shows "a complete disregard" for authentic recitation; it called on Egypt's legislature to pass laws "to regulate the use of artificial intelligence in dealing with religious texts and sanctities" — [Egypt Independent, 21 September 2024](https://www.egyptindependent.com/?p=2782519). Kind: an institutional statement, not a fatwa.
- **IslamWeb, fatwa 510187, 20 March 2025 (Tarteel AI):** "The default ruling in this matter is permissibility"; "Artificial intelligence programs are still in their early stages and are prone to many errors"; allowed for memorisation and correction when "a Qur'an-teaching program is endorsed by qualified experts in Qur'anic recitation as being free from errors"; "if the Quran should not be learned from a person who makes mistakes, it is more fitting not to learn it from a program" — [IslamWeb 510187](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/510187/is-using-tarteel-ai-permissible). Kind: a fatwa. Reading: error-freedom certified by experts is the condition; a generator cannot be certified error-free, a lookup can [inference].
- **IslamQA, answer 512399, 21 September 2024 (AI voice reciting the Quran):** permissible only with the voice owner's consent, for a permissible purpose and without harm; the owner "does not earn reward for reciting the Quran" he did not recite; cites 5:3, Sahih Muslim 2674 and al-Nawawi's Sharh Muslim 16/226 — [IslamQA 512399](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/512399). Kind: a fatwa (on consent, not on text accuracy).
- **OIC International Islamic Fiqh Academy, Resolution 258 (3/26), 26th session, Doha, 4–8 May 2025 (page dated 20 November 2025):** "The default ruling regarding the development and use of artificial intelligence is permissibility (ibāhah), subject to the following ethical and legal conditions", including that it "must not insult or abuse beliefs, religions, or religious symbols" and "must ensure protection of information and preserve both public and private rights"; it references ICESCO's Riyadh Charter on AI (2025) and recommends a study on AI legal personality and specialised symposia; no clause on the Quran, Sunnah, training or generation — [IIFA Resolution 258](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/56035.html). Kind: a resolution (collective ruling).

## Key question 2 — Where the line between input and target lies, what it means for each code path, and what "Quranic root-space" may honestly mean

### Takeaway
The repository currently makes the Quran a next-byte **target** in two places (the `continuation` path, "Real targets: Exact original Arabic bytes", and the 0.30 `quran` mixing weight of `train_full.py`) while its own Tadbir layer treats the Quran only as a **lookup source**: Fihris is BM25 over the ayahs, Iqtibas is exact-span lookup with a background bigram model fitted on *ordinary speech* (Lughah corpora + Qasd conversation seeds), and the only statistical model fitted on any Quran-derived text is a letter model of the English translation's *vocabulary* that classifies spelling and emits no text. The policy that follows from KQ1 and the licences is: Quran as **input** (labels, keys, ids, indexes) is allowed; Quran as **target** (any loss that rewards reproducing its bytes or words) is forbidden; "Quranic root-space" may only mean "a root inventory catalogued from Quranic Arabic morphology, used as lexicon ids" — and today it does not even mean that, because Bayan's roots are a hand-authored list of 55.

### Cited Findings

- Memorisation literature: memorisation of training sequences "significantly grows" with model capacity, with the number of times a sequence is duplicated, and with the prompt's context length, each log-linearly — [Carlini et al., "Quantifying Memorization Across Neural Language Models", arXiv 2202.07646, Feb 2022 (v3 Mar 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07646). The Goldfish loss (dropping a pseudorandom subset of tokens from the next-token loss) gives "significant reductions in extractable memorization" with "little to no impact on downstream benchmarks" on billion-scale Llama-2 models — [Hans et al., arXiv 2406.10209, June 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10209). MemFree decoding (a Bloom filter over training-set n-grams that removes any token completing a memorised n-gram; 1.6 GB for the Pile at the chosen n, "does not impact downstream model performance") prevents verbatim regurgitation, but approximate memorisation (BLEU > 0.75 with the true continuation) is up to twice as common as exact, and "style-transfer" prompts (comments, translation, whitespace) recover memorised content past the filter — [Ippolito et al., arXiv 2210.17546, Oct 2022 (v3 Sep 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17546).

## Key question 3 — Measured canary: do byte models trained with the mushaf as a target emit near-Quranic altered strings, does Sidq catch them, and does a model that never saw the Quran produce Quran-like strings?

### Takeaway
**Measured by the coordinator, 30 Oct 2026 (one seed per arm).** Two 1.90M-parameter byte LMs trained on equal tokens (5.58M; 1,211 steps × 12 × 384): arm a on an Arabic Wikipedia sample only, arm b with 30 % Tanzil Uthmani Hafs (training surah components only, ≈1.47 epochs of that text) and 70 % the same Wikipedia. Prompted with the first half of 50 ayahs (20 from held-out surah components, 30 from training surahs), 5 samples each: **neither arm reproduced any ayah (0/250 exact) and neither produced a near-copy (0/250 at normalised edit distance ≤ 0.20; 95 % upper bound ≈1.5 %)**. What arm b did learn is the *register*: its log-loss on the true continuation fell from 2.06 to 1.00 nats per byte (held-out surahs 2.10 → 1.03, so style, not memorised ayahs), Carlini exposure rose from 1.4–1.5 to 2.4–2.6 bits (max 5.8, never rank 1 of 1,024), and **99.2 % of its continuations were written in Uthmani mushaf orthography (ٱ, superscript alif, Quranic pause marks) against 0 % for arm a**, as invented words with Quranic morphology (e.g. «ٱلْعَيْمِكُونَ», «ٱلْأَوْكَآرُونَ», «ٱلْأَعْبَلُونَ»). At this size the danger is not a garbled ayah but **text that looks like the mushaf and is not** — the appearance 3:78 describes ("so you may think it is from the Scripture, but it is not"). Memorisation grows with capacity and repetition, so near-copies at 5–15M parameters and more epochs remain expected and unmeasured.

### Cited Findings
- **[measured]** `canary/runs/canary_eval.json/summary.json`: arm a exact 0.0, mean normalised edit distance 0.868 (folded 0.750), near-copies ≤ 0.20: 0; arm b exact 0.0, mean NED 0.754 (folded 0.757), near-copies: 0. Held vs train surahs indistinguishable in both arms (b: 0.747 vs 0.759).
- **[measured]** Target log-loss per byte (nats): a held 2.103 / train 2.026; b held 1.027 / train 0.989.
- **[measured]** Exposure (`exposure.json`, |R| = 1,024 perturbed candidates per ayah, 1–3 words replaced with training-vocabulary words of similar length): planted ayahs a 1.54 / b 2.38 mean bits (median rank 347 vs 192); held-out ayahs a 1.38 / b 2.62 (median rank 373 vs 184, max 5.83 bits); rank 1 never reached.
- **[measured]** Uthmani-script marks (U+0671, U+0670, U+06D6–U+06ED) in continuations: a 0/250 (Wilson 0–1.5 %), b 248/250 (97.1–99.8 %).
- **[measured]** MIZAN's quote check, given the true reference, labelled every non-exact continuation `differs` (a 249, b 245) or `close` (a 1, b 5); none `matches` or `excerpt`. At this distance the check catches everything; its measured blindness (KQ2: one changed word in 2:255 → `matches`; no check without a reference) concerns near-copies, which these models did not produce.
- **[measured]** No evaluated ayah or target half appears in the Wikipedia sample (overlap checks 0/0/0), so arm a's numbers are a true no-Quran baseline.

### Inferences
- **[inference]** The policy "the Quran is never a training target" does not rest on this canary: it rests on the DIRECT output-side rulings (KQ1), the licences and the owner's rule. The canary adds a concrete, measured harm even at 2M parameters: a target-trained model writes invented words in mushaf script, so any Ruh output must be checked for Uthmani marks and Quranic n-grams *without* a reference (the decode-time 16-gram guard catches copies, not this; a script check on generated text does).
- **[inference]** The canary is now a baseline for the Imtihan scripture-canary block: a Ruh checkpoint passes only if its exact and near-copy rates and its Uthmani-mark rate sit within arm a's Wilson intervals (0–1.5 %) and its exposure within ±1 bit of arm a's.

### Gaps
- One seed per arm, 1.9M parameters, ≈1.5 epochs of the Quran; the scale where near-copies appear was not reached. 50 ayahs × 5 samples.
- Exposure uses perturbed candidates, not Carlini's random canaries, so it measures preference for the true wording over plausible alternatives.

## Key question 4 — Design consequences (checklist for the report writer)

### Takeaway
Take the Quran out of every loss, keep it in every index: Ruh's mix becomes 0 % scripture with a collator-enforced target mask for any scripture that appears as input; roots stay as QAC-catalogued lexicon ids; memory rows and datastores hold references, not ayah strings; Imtihan gets a scripture-canary block in which the model must *fail* to reproduce scripture while Fihris must reproduce it byte-for-byte; Sidq's thresholds and reference requirement are tightened; the README stops saying "Quran 0.30" and "root-space" stops implying "learned the Quran".

### Cited Findings
- The code points each item changes are those cited in KQ2 (pipeline defaults, ratios, training paths, roots module, Fihris/Iqtibas, Sidq/Muhasaba thresholds and reference rule) and the measured Sidq behaviours (one-word and diacritic-only alterations pass as "matches"; unreferenced Arabic is unchecked) [measured].
- Cheap guards exist in the literature: an n-gram Bloom filter over the protected corpus removes any token that would complete a protected n-gram at decode time without hurting downstream performance ([Ippolito et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17546)); the protected corpus here is 1,327,393 bytes (6,236 ayahs, mean 213 bytes) [measured], so an exact set of all Quranic word 4-grams or byte 16-grams fits in a few megabytes, no Bloom filter needed [estimated]; loss masking of protected tokens is the training-time counterpart ([Hans et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10209)); approximate (near-verbatim) regurgitation evades exact filters and must be measured by edit distance ([Ippolito et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17546)).
- Fiqh basis for "correct or destroy, never circulate": the Permanent Committee's nos. 6252/176/8381 and IslamWeb 510508 (KQ1 §F) — [Salafy Ink translation](https://salafyink.substack.com/p/what-should-be-done-with-a-mushaf), [IslamWeb 510508](https://www.islamweb.net/en/printfatwa.php?id=510508).

### Inferences (the checklist)
1. **Ruh data mix.** `train_full.py` `_DEFAULT_RATIOS`: `quran: 0.0`, `hadith: 0.0`; `pipeline.py` `DEFAULT_SOURCES`: drop `quran` as a `text` domain; `docs/quranic_training_paths.md`: delete the `continuation` objective (or relabel it "evaluation-only canary"); keep `morphology` (labels as targets, scripture bytes masked) and `grounded_qa` (Pickthall PD, quoted span masked). Replace `training_permission` by a per-column `targets_allowed: false` enforced in `RuhCollator` and asserted by a test that scans a training epoch for any supervised byte inside an ayah span. [inference]
2. **Roots.** QAC-derived root ids may stay as the lexicon (personal, self-hosted; QAC GPL + non-commercial FAQ; no ayah text stored with them; provenance per id). The current 55-root hand list is not "Quranic root-space" and should be replaced by the Qāmūs build before the name is used. [inference]
3. **kNN datastore / Hifz / Ta'allum / Daftar rows holding ayahs.** Store `(surah:ayah, edition, sha256 of the edition's ayah)` and render by lookup at answer time; a row that must keep text for display (e.g. a Ta'allum wording with a quoted ayah) is hash-checked against the pinned edition before display and dropped (`hifz.drop`-style) on mismatch; such rows are excluded from every training export (`learner/data_export.py`) and from any Ruh/Ta'allum fit. The kNN-LM interpolation form is not built. [inference]
4. **Imtihan scripture-canary block.** (a) *Weights must not know scripture*: for N ayahs (stratified by length, including surahs never used anywhere in training), prompt with the first half; pass iff exact-continuation rate and the near-altered rate (0 < normalised edit distance ≤ 0.20) are within the no-Quran baseline's Wilson interval, and iff Carlini exposure on planted ayahs is within ±1 bit of the control ayahs; (b) *Fihris must know it*: every reference lookup returns a byte-identical string to the pinned edition (SHA-256 per ayah); (c) *garbling detector on any model output*: an Arabic run that shares a Quranic word 4-gram but is not an exact substring of an ayah is a finding, with or without a reference; (d) *Sidq thresholds*: "matches" requires coverage = precision = 1.0 on folded words and, when the source carries diacritics, byte equality of the marks; "close/excerpt" never silence a changed word; an unreferenced Arabic run matching ≥ 4 consecutive Quranic words is checked against its nearest ayah (Iqtibas already finds the span). [inference]
5. **Decode-time guard for Ruh (cheap on CPU).** Keep the set of all Quranic byte 16-grams (≈1.3 M entries [estimated]) and, in Ruh's sampler, zero the probability of any byte that would complete one unless the generation is a Fihris lookup; this is MemFree with an exact set instead of a Bloom filter, and it turns "the model cannot emit scripture from weights" from a hope into a property. It does not stop near-verbatim output (Ippolito's finding), so (4c) stays. [inference]
