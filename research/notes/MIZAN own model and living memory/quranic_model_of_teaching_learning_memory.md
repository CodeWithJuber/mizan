# The Quranic model of teaching, learning, retention, forgetting and correction, mapped to MIZAN's CPU-only learning stack

Researcher notes, 29 October 2026. Scope: Quran and authentic Sunnah on how Allah taught humankind and how humans learn, remember, forget and are corrected; classical tafsir and classical pedagogy (al-Ghazali, Ibn Khaldun, the hifz tradition); each item mapped to (a) a mechanism MIZAN can run on 2 vCPU / 2 GB with no model training spend and (b) the mathematics it suggests.

Method note. Every Quran translation below is Saheeh International, fetched verbatim from quran.com's canonical service (edition `en-sahih-international`, link suffix `?translations=20`). Tafsir was fetched from quran.com editions: Ibn Kathir (abridged English, `en-ibn-kathir`), al-Sa'di (`ar-saadi`), al-Tabari (`ar-tabari`), al-Qurtubi (`ar-qurtubi`); renderings of the Arabic editions are model translations and are marked "(model tr.)". Hadith texts come from the fawazahmed0 hadith-api editions (English, with graders' verdicts where the edition carries them; Bukhari and Muslim carry no grader field because inclusion in the Sahih is the grade) and, for Sahih Muslim, from the `eng-muslim.min.json` edition searched by text (sunnah.com itself returned HTTP 403 to this session). sunnah.com numbers are given for cross-reference.

Labels for the honesty of each mapping:
- **DIRECT**: the text itself states the principle (e.g. 2:106 "better than it or similar to it").
- **TAFSIR**: a fetched classical tafsir states the pedagogical or epistemic reading used here.
- **ISHARA**: an indication by analogy. MIZAN may be designed in its light, but the verse does not legislate the mechanism, and the report must say so.

## Key question 1. For each verse and hadith: exact text, what classical tafsir says, the honest strength of the mapping, and the mechanism and formula it suggests

### Takeaway

The Quran describes a learner who is taught names (grounded symbols) before it can speak about them, receives knowledge in paced instalments it must hear out before answering, is promised retention of what Allah preserves and told that its own forgetting is not blamed but must be repaired by remembrance, keeps an append-only record while a revisable layer is erased or confirmed above it, replaces only with equal-or-better, accepts testimony only from two independent witnesses or one of proven weight, asks the knowledgeable when it does not know, and stands under an unbounded ladder of "one more knowing". Most of these are DIRECT or TAFSIR-supported as epistemic principles; their translation into Beta posteriors, FSRS stability, e-value gates and hash chains is MIZAN's own inference and is labelled ISHARA throughout.

#### A. Teaching, grounding and expression (ta'lim, bayan, the pen)

Then He showed them to the angels and said, 'Inform Me of the names of these, if you are truthful.'" / "They said, 'Exalted are You; we have no knowledge except what You have taught us. Indeed, it is You who is the Knowing, the Wise.'" / "He said, 'O Adam, inform them of their names.' - Ibn Kathir on 2:31: Ibn Abbas (via al-Dahhak) glosses the names as "the names that people use, such as human, animal, sky, earth, land, sea, horse, donkey, and so forth, including the names of the other species"; Ibn Kathir sums up that "Allah taught Adam the names of everything, their proper names, the names of their characteristics, and what they do"; Qatadah: "Allah paraded the objects before the angels". The angels' reply means "no creature could ever acquire any part of Allah's knowledge, except by His permission, nor could anyone know anything except what Allah teaches them." — [Ibn Kathir on 2:31](https://quran.com/2:31/tafsirs/169)
- Al-Tabari on 2:31 (model tr.) records the three early views (names of everything; names of the angels; names of Adam's descendants) and, on the grammatical ground that the pronoun in "showed them" (ʿaraḍahum) is the one Arabic uses for rational beings, prefers "the names of his descendants and of the angels", while allowing Ibn Abbas's "everything" under Ubayy's reading; he closes that Allah showed the angels the limit of their knowledge even of present, visible creatures, "so do not ask Me about that of which you have no knowledge", and praises the angels' immediate return to the truth. — [al-Tabari on 2:31](https://quran.com/2:31/tafsirs/15)

Mechanism: Bayan's `(root_id, pattern_id)` symbols are MIZAN's "names"; 2:33 (Adam informs the angels) is the pattern "taught once by the teacher, then the learner transmits" that the owner wants for the base model (ISHARA).

Ibn Kathir: al-Hasan glossed al-bayan as "Eloquent speech", and Allah "made memorizing and understanding of it easy for those on whom He has bestowed His mercy", teaching "the servants how to recite it by making it easy for them to speak and pronounce letters". — [Ibn Kathir on 55:1–4](https://quran.com/55:1/tafsirs/169). Strength: that teaching of the Quran precedes and is distinct from the faculty of expression is DIRECT in the verse order; "Lisan (expression) is a separate faculty from knowledge" is ISHARA.

#### B. Paced reception, repetition and gradualness (tadarruj, tikrar)

- 20:114: "So high [above all] is Allāh, the Sovereign, the Truth. Al-Sa'di (model tr.): do not rush to seize the Quran while Jibril recites it; wait until he finishes, for Allah has guaranteed its collection in your breast; the Prophet's eagerness showed his love of knowledge, so he was told to ask for more; "the path to it is striving, longing for knowledge, asking Allah and depending on Him"; and the verse yields "the etiquette of receiving knowledge: the listener should be patient until the dictating teacher finishes his connected speech, then ask; cutting in is a cause of deprivation; likewise the one asked should take in the questioner's question and know what is meant before answering, for that is a cause of hitting the right answer." — [al-Sa'di on 20:114](https://quran.com/20:114/tafsirs/91). Strength: "ask for increase" (open-ended learning) is DIRECT; "listen to the whole before answering; understand the question before answering" is TAFSIR; mapping to "never judge a stream before it closes; Fahm must settle the intent before planning" is ISHARA.

#### C. Retention, forgetting and remembrance (hifz, nisyan, tadhkir)

- 87:6–7: "We will make you recite, [O Muḥammad], and you will not forget," / "Except what Allāh should will. Indeed, He knows what is declared and what is hidden." — [Quran 87:6–7](https://quran.com/87:6-7?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: "This is Allah informing and promising him (the Prophet) that He will teach him a recitation that he will not forget." Qatadah: "The Prophet did not forget anything except what Allah willed." A second view: the exception is "that which would be abrogated … which He removes and there is no sin on your leaving it off (not retaining it)." On 87:9 ("remind in case the reminder profits") Ibn Kathir derives "the etiquette of spreading knowledge, that it should not be wasted upon those who are not suitable or worthy of it", quoting Ali: "You do not tell people any statement that their intellects do not grasp except that it will be a Fitnah (trial) for some of them." — [Ibn Kathir on 87:1–13](https://quran.com/87:1/tafsirs/169). Al-Tabari (model tr.) adds Mujahid: the Prophet "used to rehearse the Quran within himself for fear of forgetting", and prefers the reading "you will not forget, except what We will to make you forget by abrogating and lifting it". — [al-Tabari on 87:6](https://quran.com/87:6/tafsirs/15), [87:7](https://quran.com/87:7/tafsirs/15). Strength: "what Allah preserves is not forgotten; forgetting happens only by His will (abrogation)" is DIRECT plus TAFSIR; "forgetting as policy" is ISHARA: the only sanctioned forgetting of revealed text is deliberate replacement (2:106), which for MIZAN means canonical text is never a memory to decay but a source to look up, and intentional removal is a receipted act (Mahw), not decay.
- 18:23–24: "And never say of anything, 'Indeed, I will do that tomorrow,'" / "Except [when adding], 'If Allāh wills.' And remember your Lord when you forget [it] and say, 'Perhaps my Lord will guide me to what is nearer than this to right conduct.'" — [Quran 18:23–24](https://quran.com/18:23-24?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: the verse was revealed when the Prophet, asked about the People of the Cave, said "I will tell you tomorrow" and "the revelation was delayed for fifteen days"; "remember your Lord when you forget" means "if you forget to say 'If Allah wills', then say it when you remember" (Abu al-Aliyah, al-Hasan; Ibn Abbas: "even if it is a year later"); and the last clause means "if you (O Prophet) are asked about something you know nothing about, ask Allah about it, and turn to Him so that He may guide you to what is right." — [Ibn Kathir on 18:23–24](https://quran.com/18:23/tafsirs/169). Strength: forgetting is expected and the remedy is remembrance when it returns (DIRECT + TAFSIR); "a promise about tomorrow must carry a condition" (DIRECT). Mechanism (ISHARA): a lapse is not a terminal state; the system records the lapse and re-attaches the item when a cue returns (late recall is still recall); every forward-looking statement MIZAN makes about its own future action is conditional and must not be asserted as certain.

#### E. Verification and witnesses (tathabbut, two witnesses, the pinned corpus)

- 49:6 is covered in the earlier report (al-Tabari's fa-tathabbatu; Ibn Kathir on narrators of unknown reliability). Text re-fetched: "O you who have believed, if there comes to you a disobedient one with information, investigate, lest you harm a people out of ignorance and become, over what you have done, regretful." — [Quran 49:6](https://quran.com/49:6?translations=20).
- Two independent witnesses in practice. Bukhari 6245: Abu Musa reported the Prophet's rule about asking permission three times; Umar said "By Allah! We will ask Abu Musa to bring witnesses for it"; Abu Sa'id al-Khudri went "and informed Umar that the Prophet (ﷺ) had said so" — [Bukhari 6245](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/6245.json). Abu Dawud 2894 / Tirmidhi 2101: a grandmother asked Abu Bakr for her share; he said "There is nothing prescribed for you in Allah's Book, nor do I know anything for you in the Sunnah … Go home till I question the people"; al-Mughirah testified to a sixth; "AbuBakr said: Is there anyone with you? Muhammad ibn Maslamah stood and said the same" — graded Da'if by al-Albani and Abd al-Hamid but Hasan Sahih by al-Arna'ut (and Bashar Awad Ma'ruf at Tirmidhi) and Sahih by Zubair Ali Zai ([Abu Dawud 2894](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-abudawud/2894.json), [Tirmidhi 2101](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-tirmidhi/2101.json)). Strength: Companion practice of demanding a second witness before acting is DIRECT for Bukhari 6245 (sahih) and disputed-grade for 2894/2101.
- A witness of proven weight counts as two. Bukhari 4784 (Zayd ibn Thabit): "When we collected the fragmentary manuscripts of the Qur'an into copies, I missed one of the Verses of Surat al-Ahzab which I used to hear Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) reading. Finally I did not find it with anybody except Khuza`ima Al-Ansari, whose witness was considered by Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) equal to the witness of two men." — [Bukhari 4784](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/4784.json). Umar's rule that "nothing would be accepted from anyone until two witnesses testify to it" during Abu Bakr's collection is quoted by a secondary site from Ibn Hajar's Fath al-Bari without grading ([aboutislam.net summary](https://aboutislam.net/?p=149176)); the primary chain (Ibn Abi Dawud, Kitab al-Masahif) was not verified in this session.
- Pinning the canonical corpus. Bukhari 4679/4986: Abu Bakr commissioned Zayd ("You are a wise young man and we do not suspect you (of telling lies or of forgetfulness): and you used to write the Divine Inspiration") to collect the Quran — [Bukhari 4679](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/4679.json). Bukhari 4987: Uthman "ordered Zaid bin Thabit, `Abdullah bin Az-Zubair, Sa`id bin Al-As and `AbdurRahman bin Harith bin Hisham to rewrite the manuscripts in perfect copies … `Uthman sent to every Muslim province one copy of what they had copied, and ordered that all the other Qur'anic materials, whether written in fragmentary manuscripts or whole copies, be burnt" — [Bukhari 4987](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/4987.json). Bukhari 5041 (Umar and Hisham): both recitations of al-Furqan were confirmed "It was revealed to be recited in this way", and "The Qur'an has been revealed to be recited in several different ways, so recite of it that which is easier for you" — [Bukhari 5041](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/5041.json). - Bukhari 4999/5000: "Take (learn) the Qur'an from four: `Abdullah bin Masud, Salim, Mu`adh and Ubai bin Ka`b"; Ibn Mas'ud: "I learnt over seventy Suras direct from Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)" — [Bukhari 4999](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/4999.json), [Bukhari 5000](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/5000.json). #### F. Asking, escalation and the ladder of knowers

- 16:43: "… So ask the people of the message [i.e., former scriptures] if you do not know." — [Quran 16:43](https://quran.com/16:43?translations=20). Al-Qurtubi (model tr.): Sufyan: "the believers of the People of the Book"; Ibn Abbas: "the people of the Quran"; others: "the people of knowledge"; "the meaning is close." — [al-Qurtubi on 16:43](https://quran.com/16:43/tafsirs/90). Strength: the context is asking about earlier messengers (Ibn Kathir, earlier report); al-Qurtubi's "ahl al-ilm, the meanings are close" gives TAFSIR cover for a general "ask the knowledgeable", but MIZAN's use for web sources stays ISHARA.
- 12:76: "… We raise in degrees whom We will, but over every possessor of knowledge is one [more] knowing." — [Quran 12:76](https://quran.com/12:76?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: al-Hasan: "There is no knowledgeable person, but there is another person with more knowledge until it ends at Allah the Exalted"; Qatadah: "knowledge started from Allah, and from Him the scholars learn, and to Him all knowledge returns"; Ibn Abbas rebuked a man who said "There is an all-knowing above every person endowed with knowledge" with "Allah is the All-Knowing and His knowledge is above the knowledge of every knowledgeable person." — [Ibn Kathir on 12:76](https://quran.com/12:73/tafsirs/169). Al-Qurtubi (model tr.): "We raise in degrees whom We will: by knowledge and faith"; Ibn Abbas: "this one is more knowing than that one, and Allah is above every knower." — [al-Qurtubi on 12:76](https://quran.com/12:76/tafsirs/90). Strength: an unbounded ladder of knowers ending in Allah is DIRECT + TAFSIR; "an escalation rung always exists, and the top rung is 'Allah knows best'" (abstention) is ISHARA.
- 18:65–70, 78: "And they found a servant from among Our servants … [whom We] had taught him from Us a [certain] knowledge." / "Moses said to him, 'May I follow you on [the condition] that you teach me from what you have been taught of sound judgement?'" / "He said, 'Indeed, with me you will never be able to have patience.'" / "And how can you have patience for what you do not encompass in knowledge?" / "[Moses] said, 'You will find me, if Allāh wills, patient, and I will not disobey you in [any] order.'" / "He said, 'Then if you follow me, do not ask me about anything until I make to you about it mention [i.e., explanation].'" … "[Al-Khiḍr] said, 'This is parting between me and you. I will inform you of the interpretation of that about which you could not have patience.'" — [Quran 18:65–70](https://quran.com/18:65-70?translations=20), [18:78](https://quran.com/18:78?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: Khidr "was one to whom Allah had given knowledge that He had not given to Musa, just as He had given Musa knowledge that He had not given to Al-Khidr"; Musa's question "is phrased in gentle terms, with no sense of force or coercion. This is the manner in which the seeker of knowledge should address the scholar"; Khidr's condition: "do not initiate any discussion of the matter … until I initiate the discussion, before you ask me about it." — [Ibn Kathir on 18:66–68](https://quran.com/18:66/tafsirs/169). Strength: complementary specialisation of knowers, learner's patience and the teacher's right to set terms and to end the session are DIRECT; mapping to Majlis specialists and bounded teacher sessions is ISHARA.
- Bukhari 125 (Ibn Mas'ud): the Jews asked "What is the spirit?" "The Prophet (ﷺ) remained quiet. I thought he was being inspired Divinely. So I stayed till that state … was over. The Prophet (ﷺ) then said, 'And they ask you (O Muhammad) concerning the spirit --Say: The spirit -- its knowledge is with my Lord. And of knowledge you (mankind) have been given only a little.'" — [Bukhari 125](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/eng-bukhari/125.json). Strength: silence until the source answers, instead of guessing — DIRECT.

#### G. Degrees of knowledge, certainty and the duty not to claim without evidence

- 39:9: "… Say, 'Are those who know equal to those who do not know?' Only they will remember [who are] people of understanding." — [Quran 39:9](https://quran.com/39:9?translations=20). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): they are not equal "as night and day, light and darkness, water and fire are not equal"; the people of understanding "prefer the higher to the lower, knowledge to ignorance". — [al-Sa'di on 39:9](https://quran.com/39:9/tafsirs/91). Al-Qurtubi (model tr.): "those who know are those who benefit from their knowledge and act on it; as for one who does not benefit from his knowledge and does not act on it, he is in the position of one who does not know." — [al-Qurtubi on 39:9](https://quran.com/39:9/tafsirs/90). Strength: degrees exist is DIRECT; "unused knowledge is as if not known" is TAFSIR (al-Qurtubi), which supports usefulness-weighted retention.
- 58:11: "… Allāh will raise those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge, by degrees. …" — [Quran 58:11](https://quran.com/58:11?translations=20). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): Allah raises the people of knowledge and faith "in degrees according to what He granted them of knowledge and faith"; "the virtue of knowledge, whose adornment and fruit is to be mannered by its manners and to act by what it requires." — [al-Sa'di on 58:11](https://quran.com/58:11/tafsirs/91). DIRECT (degrees); the Isnad grade ladder is ISHARA.
- 17:36 (earlier report). 2:260: Ibrahim asked "My Lord, show me how You give life to the dead." [Allāh] said, "Have you not believed?" He said, "Yes, but [I ask] only that my heart may be satisfied." — [Quran 2:260](https://quran.com/2:260?translations=20). 6:75–79: Ibrahim is shown the realm "that he would be among the certain [in faith]", then rejects the star, moon and sun in turn: "I like not those that set [i.e., disappear]" … "Unless my Lord guides me, I will surely be among the people gone astray" … "Indeed, I have turned my face [i.e., self] toward He who created the heavens and the earth". — [Quran 6:75–79](https://quran.com/6:75-79?translations=20). Strength: certainty is sought by evidence even by a believer, and hypotheses that fail an observation are discarded — DIRECT as narrative; "hypothesis elimination loop" is ISHARA (no tafsir fetched for 6:75–79).
- 102:5–7, 69:51 (three grades of certainty) are in the earlier report; texts re-fetched: "No! If you only knew with knowledge of certainty..." / "Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty." / "And indeed, it is the truth of certainty." — [Quran 102:5](https://quran.com/102:5?translations=20), [102:7](https://quran.com/102:7?translations=20), [69:51](https://quran.com/69:51?translations=20).
- 103:1–3: "By time," / "Indeed, mankind is in loss," / "Except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience." (Saheeh International text as fetched) — [Quran 103:1–3](https://quran.com/103:1-3?translations=20). Ibn Kathir: "Al-`Asr is the time in which the movements of the Children of Adam occur, whether good or evil"; al-Shafi'i: "If the people were to ponder on this Surah, it would be sufficient for them"; two Companions "would not part until one of them had recited Surat Al-`Asr in its entirety to the other" (al-Tabarani). — [Ibn Kathir on 103](https://quran.com/103:1/tafsirs/169). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): time is "the locus of the servants' acts and deeds"; faith "is not without knowledge; it is a branch of it that is not complete except by it"; "by the first two matters a person perfects himself, and by the last two he perfects others." — [al-Sa'di on 103:1–3](https://quran.com/103:1/tafsirs/91). Strength: time as the scarce, loss-bearing resource and mutual counsel (tawasi) as a duty are DIRECT; "latency and decay budgets; peer verification" are ISHARA.

#### H. Correction: insight after a slip, self-audit, repentance and replacement

- 7:201: "Indeed, those who fear Allāh - when an impulse touches them from Satan, they remember [Him] and at once they have insight." — [Quran 7:201](https://quran.com/7:201?translations=20). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): the God-fearing person, when he senses a sin, "remembers from which door he was approached and by which entrance Satan entered upon him, remembers what Allah obliged him and what faith requires, so he sees and seeks Allah's forgiveness and makes good what escaped him with sincere repentance and many good deeds, and sends his devil back disgraced." — [al-Sa'di on 7:201](https://quran.com/7:201/tafsirs/91). - 59:18: "O you who have believed, fear Allāh. ### Inferences

- The Quran's own preservation model splits into two regimes that MIZAN should keep architecturally separate: (i) revealed text, where retention is guaranteed by the Source and forgetting occurs only by deliberate abrogation (87:6–7 with al-Tabari's preferred reading; 2:106), and (ii) human learning, where forgetting is expected, forgiven and repaired by cue and rehearsal (18:24; 2:286; Bukhari 5032–5038). Regime (i) maps to pinned, hash-verified sources that are always looked up (Fihris, hadith editions, Sarf corpus) and never summarised into a decaying memory; regime (ii) maps to Tadhkir's FSRS/ACT-R stores. Mixing them (letting a model "remember" an ayah) violates (i). This is MIZAN's inference; the verses do not legislate storage design.
- "Two witnesses, or one of proven weight" (2:282; Bukhari 4784) gives a cleaner rule than a flat two-source requirement: accept when Σ_i log((1−p_i)/p_i) over independent witnesses clears a harm-scaled threshold, where p_i is the witness's learned error rate. Khuzaimah's testimony counting as two is exactly a witness whose p_i is small enough to clear the bar alone. Ahl al-Dhikr's per-site Beta reliability already supplies p_i; the inference is to replace "two sites agree" with the additive log-odds rule and keep the independence requirement (different sites, different days) explicit, since the p₁p₂ bound holds only under independence.
- Bukhari 6133 argues against treating every failure the same way. For harmless mistakes, forgetting is forgiven and a posterior may update slowly (2:286; Ibn Majah 2045). For a confirmed harmful outcome (an injected instruction obeyed, a file deleted, a fabricated citation shown), the believer "is not stung twice": the correct learner is a hard constraint with probability 0 of repetition after a single observation, not a Beta(1 + n_fail, …) that needs many samples. MIZAN already has this shape in Hudud and Amanah's pins; the inference is to route Tajriba lessons by failure signature into deterministic guards whenever the failure was harmful, and into evidence-weighted lessons otherwise.
- Al-Sa'di's adab on 20:114 and 75:16–19 (do not pre-empt the teacher; understand the question before answering) matches two existing engineering facts and suggests a third: Fahm decides on the complete message; Furqan judges a finished answer; and the base model's streamed output should not be acted on by any tool until the stream closes (tool arguments parsed mid-stream are the machine analogue of "moving the tongue to hasten").
- Bukhari 5054 and Abu Dawud 1394 give the only Sunnah-grounded numbers for a review cycle: the whole memorised corpus between 7 and 30 days, never faster than 3 days. Under the FSRS-4.5 form R = (1 + t/(9S))^(−1), a weekly cycle keeps R ≥ 0.9 only for items with stability S ≥ 7 days, and an item with S = 7 days tested after 30 days has R ≈ 0.68 (computed in this session). The inference for MIZAN's learned, non-canonical items (Hifz programs, Ta'allum wording, Daftar facts) is to schedule the next check by I(r,S) and to treat 7 d and 30 d as the prior bounds on target stability before an item is called "settled". This is ISHARA: the hadith concern recitation of the Quran, not software caches.
- The "ten verses" practice (Ahmad 23482, hasan) and Aisha's order of legislation (Bukhari 4993) describe a curriculum with a mastery gate: no new batch until the previous is known and practised, and easier/motivating material first. For Ta'lim batches and Lughah seeding this argues for small batches accepted one at a time with a verification step on use (not only on classification accuracy), and for seeding frequent, low-risk intents before rare, high-risk ones. The 28 October report's D1 (row-valued batches with an undo) is compatible; the extension is the "action verified" half of the gate.
- 12:76 with Ibn Abbas's correction supplies the end of the Wazn ladder: after the cheapest model, stronger models, Ahl al-Dhikr and the human, the final rung is not a bigger model but abstention with "Allah knows best" (Bukhari 4809, earlier report). A ladder that ends in a model contradicts the verse's tafsir.

## Key question 2. How the Prophet ﷺ taught, how the Companions learned, what Ibn Khaldun and al-Ghazali add, and what the hifz cycle says about spacing

### Takeaway

The authenticated record shows a teacher who repeated a sentence three times, taught by questions, by example and by the easiest-first ordering of obligations, corrected gently and without striking, reviewed the whole corpus with his own teacher yearly, bounded recitation of the whole corpus to between 3 and 30 days, and whose Companions took ten verses at a time and did not advance until they knew and practised them. Ibn Khaldun turns this into a theory of habit formed by continuous repeated activity and lost by interruption, taught in three passes of increasing depth; al-Ghazali gives eight duties of the teacher including gradualness, limiting the student to his capacity and indirect correction. The living hifz cycle (sabaq / sabqi / manzil) is a three-tier spaced schedule: new material daily, the last 7–30 days daily, and everything older cycled in seven parts, with a revision-to-new ratio of about 5:1; no controlled trial of it against spaced-repetition algorithms was found.

### Cited Findings

#### The Prophet's teaching method (authentic hadith)

#### "Knowledge is only by learning" — what it is and how strong

- The phrase وَإِنَّمَا الْعِلْمُ بِالتَّعَلُّمِ appears in the introductory material of al-Bukhari's Book of Knowledge (the chapter on knowledge preceding speech and action, which cites "فَاعْلَمْ أَنَّهُ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ" to show that Allah began with knowledge); the page gives no editorial note on whether it is the Prophet's words or al-Bukhari's — [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Knowledge page](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/bukhari/chapter/3/sub/59).
- As a chained Prophetic hadith it is Mu'awiya's narration in al-Tabarani, al-Mu'jam al-Kabir (vol. 19, no. 929): "O people, knowledge can only be acquired through seeking it …"; "Hafiz Ibn Hajar (rahimahullah) said it has been corroborated by similar narrations and is therefore sound (hasan)" (Fath al-Bari, before hadith 68; also al-Maqasid al-Hasanah 210), so "the narration is suitable to quote" — [HadithAnswers](https://hadithanswers.com/?p=14272), [IslamQA (Darul Hadith)](https://islamqa.org/?p=119800). Al-Haythami's Majma' al-Zawa'id lists the chain itself as weak (an unnamed narrator; Utbah ibn Abi Hakim disputed) — [search summary of the Majma' al-Zawa'id entry](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/majma-al-zawaid/537/). A related statement attributed to Ibn Mas'ud (mawquf) in Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah is reported graded sahih by its editor Sa'd al-Shathri — [search summary](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/musannaf-ibn-abi-shaybah/27804/). Honest status: a weak chain raised to hasan li-ghayrihi by Ibn Hajar through corroboration, used by al-Bukhari as a chapter-heading statement (ta'liq), not as a chained hadith of the Sahih.

#### How the Companions learned: ten verses, knowledge and action

- Musnad Ahmad 23482 (Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, from those who taught them the Quran): "أَنَّهُمْ كَانُوا يَقْتَرِئُونَ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ عَشْرَ آيَاتٍ فَلَا يَأْخُذُونَ فِي الْعَشْرِ الْأُخْرَى حَتَّى يَعْلَمُوا مَا فِي هَذِهِ مِنَ الْعِلْمِ وَالْعَمَلِ قَالُوا: فَعَلِمْنَا الْعِلْمَ وَالْعَمَلَ" — "They used to learn ten verses from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ [and] would not move on to the next ten until they knew what was in them of knowledge and action. Grading: Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut "إسناده حسن" (good chain); Dar al-Salam edition "إسناده حسن"; al-Haythami's caveat "فيه عطاء بن السائب اختلط في آخر عمره" — [Hadith Unlocked, Ahmad 23482](https://hadithunlocked.com/ahmad:23482), [Tohed, Musnad Ahmad](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/musnad-ahmad/chapter/1004/sub/36069/). Note the number differs by edition (a practitioner article cites it as Musnad Ahmad 22971).

These athar are quoted there without gradings and were not verified in this session; use them as colour, not as evidence.

#### The hifz cycle (sabaq / sabqi / manzil): structure and numbers

- A 2026 qualitative case study (Journal of Indonesian Islamic Studies 5(2):1011–1030, DOI 10.24256/jiis.v5i2.12297; seven informants at Rukiyah Khadizatul Kubro boarding school, North Sumatra) defines the cycle as "Saba' for adding new memorization, Sabqi for reinforcing new memorization, and Manzil for maintaining old memorization through review"; in Saba' "the memorized verses submitted by the students are assessed in terms of fluency, recitation accuracy, makhraj … and tajwid … When errors are identified, students are instructed to correct and repeat the memorization before proceeding to the next portion"; Manzil is "murāja'ah, or systematic review, of previously memorized verses … conducted regularly and accompanied by listening and correction from the ustadzah". It reports no page or day quantities and no controlled outcomes; obstacles were "time management, differences in memorization ability, fatigue, and consistency in review" — [Yasmin & Ardiansyah 2026, JIIS](https://ejournal.iainpalopo.ac.id/index.php/jiis/article/view/12297).
- A 2025 undergraduate thesis (Universitas Muhammadiyah Surakarta; Al-Ittiba' boarding school, Klaten) gives school-specific quantities: "Sabaq is a new memorization that must be submitted daily to the supervisor with a minimum of half a page"; "Sabqi is repeating new memorization that has not reached one juz of at least 5 pages"; Manzil is repeating previously submitted memorization (no quantity); evaluation by a juz-increase exam, a multiple-of-5-juz exam and a tasmi' exam — [Sofiyanah 2025, UMS eprints](https://eprints.ums.ac.id/138801).
- A practitioner "Hifz Learning Kit" (HifzMate, PDF) states the schedule most explicitly: "Sabqi (Recent Review): The material you memorized in the last 7-30 days. Review this daily"; "Manzil (Long-Term Revision): Everything you memorized more than 30 days ago. Cycle through this material systematically. A common approach: divide your completed memorization into 7 parts and review one part each day"; "A widely recommended ratio: for every 1 new page memorized, review 5 old pages"; "Start small — Begin with 3-5 ayahs per day"; a weekly structure "Saturday through Wednesday: Memorize new material (Sabaq) • Thursday: Review the week's new memorization only (Sabqi) • Friday: Full revision of all previously memorized material (Manzil)"; and on lapses: "Increase frequency — Material that you keep forgetting needs to be reviewed more often, not less" and "Re-memorize intentionally — If a section has become very weak, treat it as new memorization for a few days" — [HifzMate kit (PDF)](https://hifzweb.pages.dev/assets/pdfs/hifz-learning-kit.pdf). This is practitioner material, not research.
- A 2025 study on working-memory strategies for Quran memorisation (Jurnal Pendidikan Islam 11(2):376–389, DOI 10.15575/jpi.v11i2.44968; n = 32 students, pre-test/post-test/retention, Wilcoxon and Mann–Whitney) reports a "statistically significant difference between the mean pre-test and post-test scores", "a medium effect size", and that the post-test-to-retention decrease was "significantly smaller than the decrease between the pre-test and initial retention measurement"; the article page gives no numbers — [Dimyathi et al. 2025](https://journal.uinsgd.ac.id/index.php/jpi/article/view/44968).
- Search for 2024–2026 controlled studies of spaced repetition for hifz found only practitioner blogs and the above; a search summary notes "no 2024–2025 controlled study that directly tests spaced repetition for Qur'an memorization" — [search summary](https://tarteel.ai/blog/spaced-repetition-and-quran-memorization-how-to-make-your-hifz-stick-for-life/).

#### Ibn Khaldun, Muqaddimah, chapter VI (Rosenthal translation)

- §36 "The right attitude in scientific instruction and toward the method of giving such instruction": instruction "requires a threefold repetition" — first "the principal problems within each chapter" so the habit is "an approximate" one; second, "on a higher level", with "full commentaries and explanations" and "the existing differences of opinion"; third, when "solidly grounded", the teacher "bares all the secrets" of the discipline. "Teaching is effective only when it proceeds gradually and little by little"; teachers who begin "by confronting the student with obscure scientific problems" err, because "if one confuses a student, he will be unable to understand (anything)" and he "despairs of becoming a scholar". Long breaks make the student forget and "disrupt the nexus between the different problems"; "habits are acquired by continuous and repeated activity", and "when one forgets to act, one forgets the habit that results from that particular action". Once a student has the habit of one discipline "he is prepared for learning all the others"; two disciplines should not be studied at once — [Muqaddimah VI.36](https://www.muslimphilosophy.com/ik/Muqaddimah/Chapter6/Ch_6_36.htm).
- §38 "The instruction of children and the different methods employed in the Muslim cities": "the things one is taught in one's youth take root more deeply (than anything else)"; "The first impression the heart receives is, in a way, the foundation of (all scholarly) habits." The Maghribi method "restrict[s] the education of children to instruction in the Qur'an", so Maghribis "know the orthography of the Qur'an, and know it by heart, better than any other (Muslim group)" but "a person who knows (the Qur'an) does not acquire the habit of the Arabic language", since "no (scholarly) habit can originate from the (study of the) Qur'an, because no human being can produce anything like it"; the Spanish method adds poetry, composition and philology and yields "a better acquaintance with the Arabic language" — [Muqaddimah VI.38](https://www.muslimphilosophy.com/ik/Muqaddimah/Chapter6/Ch_6_38.htm).
- §39 "Severity to students does them harm": harsh punishment makes students "hide their true thoughts", so "deceit and trickery" become habit; Abu Muhammad ibn Abi Zayd: the educator "must not strike them more than three times"; al-Rashid's advice to Khalaf al-Ahmar: correct "as much as possible" with gentleness and "resort to severity only if gentleness fails" — [Muqaddimah VI.39](https://www.muslimphilosophy.com/ik/Muqaddimah/Chapter6/Ch_6_39.htm).
- On memorisation versus habit (Rosenthal tr., abridged Dawood, Princeton 1989, pp. 340–1): students who concentrate on memorising find, "when they enter into a discussion or disputation, or do some teaching, their scientific habit is found to be defective"; "They think that scientific habit is identical with memorized knowledge. But that is not so." — [Ibn Khaldun excerpt](https://cyh.rrchnm.org/primary-sources/461.html).

#### Al-Ghazali, Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din, Book of Knowledge, section 5 (Faris translation)

- Duties of the teacher include: (3) not to "permit him to address himself to abstruse sciences before he has mastered those which are clear"; (4) correct "by hints, not openly", because "Open dissuasion destroys the veil of awe, invites defiance, and encourages stubbornness"; (5) not to "belittle or disparage the value of other sciences" and to "observe the rules of gradual progress in promoting his students from one grade to another"; (6) to "limit the student to what the latter is able to understand", citing "No one ever relates a tradition to a people which is beyond their minds to understand" (Introduction to Sahih Muslim); (7) to give "backward students only such things as are clear and suitable to their limited understanding"; (8) to practise what he teaches, citing 2:44. Duties of the student include not addressing "at the same time … every branch of knowledge" but beginning with the most important, and "no one should address himself to one branch of knowledge before he has already mastered the branch which precedes it"; and to prefer inquiry to imitation ("One blind is not fit to lead the blind"; Ali: "Accept no truth because of the men who hold it but first find the truth") — [al-Ghazali, Book of Knowledge, section 5](https://www.ghazali.org/works/bk1-sec-5.htm).

Tirmidhi 2459 ("the clever person subjugates his soul") is Da'if by Shakir, al-Albani and Zubair Ali Zai; Umar's "reckon with yourselves" within it is an athar, not a hadith.

### Inferences

- The three tiers of the hifz cycle are a hand-tuned spaced-repetition schedule and can be written as FSRS targets (MIZAN's inference): sabaq = new item, S₀ small, reviewed the same and next day; sabqi = items aged 7–30 days reviewed daily, which under R = (1 + t/(9S))^(−1) keeps R ≥ 0.9 at t = 1 d for any S ≥ 1 d and drives S up with each successful review; manzil = items older than 30 days reviewed every 7 days, which requires S ≥ 7 d for R ≥ 0.9 and in practice holds items whose S has grown to weeks. The "1 new : 5 old pages" ratio is the steady-state cost of a daily review pass over a seven-part manzil plus sabqi: with a 604-page Quran memorised at 1 page/day (≈ 1.7 years) and a seven-day manzil, the daily manzil load alone approaches 604/7 ≈ 86 pages, so practitioners cap new intake precisely because review cost grows linearly with the corpus. For MIZAN this means the learned store must have an archive tier (D5) so that the daily re-verification budget does not grow without bound. Cepeda et al.'s optimal gap of 10–20% of the retention interval (earlier report) agrees in order of magnitude with "weekly for material you want to hold for a month or more".
- Ibn Khaldun's "when one forgets to act, one forgets the habit" is a use-dependent retention law, i.e. ACT-R's base-level activation B = ln Σ t_j^(−d) over uses, which MIZAN's Tadhkir already applies to old rows. His "threefold repetition" is coarse-to-fine curriculum: for Ta'lim and for Lisan's instructions it argues for seeding principal cases first, then differences/edge cases, then the full inventory, rather than ingesting everything once. His §38 on memorisation-only teaching yielding no language habit is a direct warning for the Ruh/Bayan plan: a model that only stores and quotes will not acquire bayan; generation must be practised and verified (Nazm, Furqan), which is why the owner's "teacher once" protocol must include MIZAN re-expressing the taught content in its own symbols before it is kept.
- Ibn Khaldun's §39 (harshness teaches students to "hide their true thoughts") and al-Ghazali's duty 4 (correct by hints) read, by analogy, as a caution on how MIZAN scores its own learners and the model: heavy penalties for admitted uncertainty drive a policy toward confident concealment; the reward should favour "I do not know" over a confident wrong answer (17:36), which is the Chow-type abstention rule in KQ1's table and the d′/ECE metrics of the earlier report's D10.
- The Prophet's questioning (Bukhari 61, 67) and "those absent might comprehend better" (Bukhari 67) give Sunnah grounding for two engineering choices: test-before-tell (retrieval practice; Roediger & Karpicke in the earlier report) when MIZAN checks a Hifz program by re-running it rather than re-reading it; and keeping a verified teacher answer for later users, who may extract more value than the first asker did (Hifz and Ta'allum across the private teams).
- Gentle correction (Bukhari 69, 220; Muslim 537) and Abu Dawud 4992 together shape MIZAN's outward corrections: Muhasaba posts a correction as a plain fact with its source, never as blame, and never relays a source's every claim.

### Gaps

- No peer-reviewed, controlled comparison of sabaq/sabqi/manzil against algorithmic spaced repetition was found; the quantitative cycle (7–30 days in sabqi, seven-part manzil, 5:1 ratio) comes from practitioner material, and the school-specific numbers (half a page/day; sabqi < 1 juz, ≥ 5 pages) from one Indonesian thesis abstract.
- The UIN Sunan Gunung Djati 2025 study's effect sizes and retention percentages were not on the article page; a search summary attributed "failure rates in memorization assessments … from 27% to 48%" and "about half of newly memorized material is initially forgotten" to its text, unverified here.
- Ibn Hajar's exact wording on "innama al-ilm bit-ta'allum" in Fath al-Bari was not read directly; the hasan-by-corroboration verdict is reported via two fatwa sites of the same institution.
- The Companions' "five verses morning and evening" (Abu Nadrah) and Ibn Umar's eight years on al-Baqarah were not graded or verified in primary sources.
- Al-Ghazali's stages of knowing (ilm al-yaqin → ayn al-yaqin → haqq al-yaqin in the Ihya'/Munqidh) were not fetched; the earlier report's al-Qurtubi reading of 102:5–7 stands in for them.
- Ibn Khaldun's exact Arabic term malakah does not appear in the Rosenthal pages fetched (rendered "habit"), and his sections were read through a web mirror of the Rosenthal translation, not a printed edition.

## Key question 3. What the Quran says about the limits of a created learner, and what that should fix in MIZAN's abstention and humility

### Takeaway

The Quran bounds the created learner four ways: knowledge is only what was taught (2:32); judgement and knowledge come with maturity (28:14); humankind has been given only a little, and the unseen (the Hour, rain, the womb, tomorrow's earning, the place of death) is withheld from all creation including prophets and angels (17:85; 31:34; 6:59; 2:255; 3:7); and the one asked may rightly refuse a vexatious question and redirect to what benefits (al-Sa'di on 17:85). For MIZAN this fixes abstention as a first-class answer, forbids confident claims about the future or the unseen, makes "Allah knows best" the top of every escalation ladder, and ties capability unlocks to demonstrated maturity.

### Cited Findings

- 2:32: "They said, 'Exalted are You; we have no knowledge except what You have taught us. …'" — [Quran 2:32](https://quran.com/2:32?translations=20); al-Tabari (model tr.) reads the whole exchange as Allah showing the angels "the shortfall of their knowledge about what they witnessed with their eyes, so how about what they had not seen or been told of?", so that they "hastened back from the slip" with "we have no knowledge except what You taught us", as Nuh did after being told "do not ask Me about that of which you have no knowledge" (11:46–47) — [al-Tabari on 2:31](https://quran.com/2:31/tafsirs/15). DIRECT (bounded knowledge; immediate retraction on being shown the bound).
- 28:14: "And when he attained his full strength and was [mentally] mature, We bestowed upon him judgement and knowledge. And thus do We reward the doers of good." — [Quran 28:14](https://quran.com/28:14?translations=20). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): "when he reached his full strength of power, intellect and understanding — mostly about forty years — and was complete in those matters, We gave him judgement by which he knows the legal rulings and judges between people, and much knowledge"; "We give them knowledge and judgement according to their ihsan". — [al-Sa'di on 28:14](https://quran.com/28:14/tafsirs/91). 26:20–21 (Musa of his earlier deed): "I did it, then, while I was of those astray [i.e., ignorant]. So I fled from you when I feared you. Then my Lord granted me judgement [i.e., wisdom and prophethood]" — [Quran 26:20–21](https://quran.com/26:20-21?translations=20). Strength: judgement is granted after maturity and in proportion to ihsan — DIRECT + TAFSIR; a developmental gate on MIZAN's riskier faculties is ISHARA.
- 17:85: "And they ask you, [O Muḥammad], about the soul. Say, 'The soul is of the affair [i.e., concern] of my Lord. And you [i.e., mankind] have not been given of knowledge except a little.'" — [Quran 17:85](https://quran.com/17:85?translations=20). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): the verse "contains a rebuke of those who ask questions meant only to vex and defeat, leaving the important question"; the ruh is "among the hidden matters whose description and nature not everyone can master, while they are deficient in the knowledge the servants need"; and "in this verse is a proof that when the one asked is asked about something while the questioner needs something else more, it is better to turn from answering it and point him to what he needs and guide him to what benefits him." — [al-Sa'di on 17:85](https://quran.com/17:85/tafsirs/91). Bukhari 125 (KQ1 §F) records the Prophet's silence until this verse came. Strength: DIRECT (little knowledge) + TAFSIR (refuse-and-redirect).
- 31:34: "Indeed, Allāh [alone] has knowledge of the Hour and sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs. And no soul perceives what it will earn tomorrow, and no soul perceives in what land it will die. Indeed, Allāh is Knowing and Aware." — [Quran 31:34](https://quran.com/31:34?translations=20). Al-Sa'di (model tr.): Allah "may let His servants know many unseen matters, but these five are among the matters whose knowledge He has folded away from all created beings, so no sent prophet knows them nor any near angel, let alone others"; "among His complete wisdom is that He hid the knowledge of these five from the servants, for in that are benefits not hidden from one who reflects." — [al-Sa'di on 31:34](https://quran.com/31:34/tafsirs/91). DIRECT.
- 6:59 ("none knows them except Him"), 2:255 ("they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except for what He wills"), 3:7 ("And no one knows its [true] interpretation except Allāh. But those firm in knowledge say, 'We believe in it. All [of it] is from our Lord.'"), 2:216 ("And Allāh knows, while you know not.") — [Quran 6:59](https://quran.com/6:59?translations=20), [2:255](https://quran.com/2:255?translations=20), [3:7](https://quran.com/3:7?translations=20), [2:216](https://quran.com/2:216?translations=20). DIRECT.
- 12:76 with Ibn Abbas's rebuke of the man who generalised "above every knower is a knower": "Allah is the All-Knowing and His knowledge is above the knowledge of every knowledgeable person" — [Ibn Kathir on 12:76](https://quran.com/12:73/tafsirs/169); al-Qurtubi records the same, "بئس ما قلت; الله العليم وهو فوق كل عالم" — [al-Qurtubi on 12:76](https://quran.com/12:76/tafsirs/90). TAFSIR: the ladder of knowers is not infinite regress among creatures; it terminates in Allah.
- 18:67–68: "Indeed, with me you will never be able to have patience." / "And how can you have patience for what you do not encompass in knowledge?" — [Quran 18:67–68](https://quran.com/18:66-70?translations=20); Ibn Kathir: "I have knowledge from Allah that He has not taught you, and you have knowledge from Allah that He has not taught me." — [Ibn Kathir on 18:66–68](https://quran.com/18:66/tafsirs/169). DIRECT: a learner cannot judge what it does not encompass; suspension of judgement is required until explanation.
- 2:286 and 20:115 (bounded capacity; Adam "forgot; and We found not in him determination") — [Quran 20:115](https://quran.com/20:115?translations=20); 2:269 ("He gives wisdom to whom He wills") — [Quran 2:269](https://quran.com/2:269?translations=20). DIRECT.
- 17:36 and Qatadah's "do not say 'I know' when you do not know" (earlier report); 87:9–10 and Ali's warning against telling people what their intellects do not grasp (Ibn Kathir, KQ1 §C). Bukhari 4809 (Ibn Mas'ud: "it is a sign of having knowledge to say about something which one does not know, 'Allah knows better'") is in the earlier report.

### Inferences

- Abstention must be a graded, first-class output with its own evaluation, not a failure mode. The texts supply three distinct abstentions that MIZAN should keep apart in `usage`/Isnad: (a) "not taught yet" (2:32) → route to Ahl al-Dhikr or the base model once, then keep; (b) "not for a creature to know" (31:34; 6:59) → refuse with the reason: predictions of the Hour, exact future events, what is in the womb, a person's death; also any question whose answer MIZAN would have to fabricate; (c) "not what you need" (al-Sa'di on 17:85) → redirect to the useful question. Each is answerable; none is a model guess. This is MIZAN's inference from the fetched tafsir, not a ruling.
- 28:14 with al-Sa'di ("knowledge and judgement according to their ihsan") grounds the existing DevelopmentalGate better than Nafs levels did: faculties that act in the world (file edits, payments, messages, rulings-adjacent answers) unlock only after a verified track record, and the threshold scales with harm. The math is the same Beta posterior as Majlis; what 28:14 adds (ISHARA) is the ordering: perception and language first (16:78), judgement last.
- 12:76 as read by Ibn Abbas forbids an escalation ladder that ends in a model. Wazn's ladder should end in the human owner and, past that, in an explicit "Allah knows best" abstention that is recorded as such; Imtihan should score that abstention as correct on items whose ground truth is "unknowable".
- 18:67–70 implies that MIZAN should not grade or overwrite a teacher's (base model's or human's) answer in a domain it does not itself encompass; Furqan/Sidq check only what MIZAN can verify (arithmetic, quotes, links, actions), and leave the rest marked da'if rather than "wrong". The earlier report's rule that assumption never overwrites a verified fact (53:28) is the complement.
- The limits also bound the owner's "universal language model": 55:4 and Ibn Khaldun §38 say expression is a faculty acquired by practice, 2:32 says knowledge is only what was taught; so a root-space interlingua can carry taught meanings across Hinglish, Urdu, Hindi, English and Arabic, but it is not a source of knowledge, and MIZAN must keep saying so in its grading.

### Gaps

- No tafsir was fetched for 3:7, 2:255, 2:216, 2:269 or 20:115; their readings here rest on the translation text.
- Classical discussion of "la adri" ("I do not know") as a mark of scholarship (sayings attributed to Malik, al-Sha'bi, Ibn Mas'ud) was not researched beyond Bukhari 4809 in the earlier report.
- The literature on machine abstention (selective prediction, conformal risk control) was out of scope for this researcher; the KQ1 table's Chow-type rule is stated without a source and should be backed by the ML researcher's notes.

## Key question 4. The mathematics in one place, and what the CPU-only, zero-spend constraint changes in the 28 October designs D1–D10

### Takeaway

Every mechanism the Quranic model suggests is non-parametric or tiny: Beta posteriors, additive log-odds for independent witnesses, FSRS/ACT-R curves, a hash chain, a paired e-value gate and a reject rule. None needs a GPU, and all already exist in some form in `core/adaptive.py`, Tadhkir, Sakk, Tajdid and Ahl al-Dhikr. The one design the new constraint invalidates is D9 (a LoRA student retrained on rented GPUs); the Quranic frame for its replacement is "taught once, then the learner informs" (2:33; Bukhari 67), implemented as keeping and re-expressing verified teacher outputs rather than fine-tuning on them.

### Cited Findings

- FSRS-6 retrievability and stability updates, ACT-R base-level activation, Cowan's four-chunk limit, Cepeda's optimal spacing (10–20% of the retention interval), the testing effect and the EM-LLM surprise rule are documented with sources in the earlier report ([28 Oct 2026 report, "Human memory science supplies working equations" and D4](/research/reports/MIZAN%20self%20learning%20architecture.md)).
- Computed in this session from the FSRS-4.5 form R(t,S) = (1 + t/(9S))^(−1): R(7 d ∣ S = 7 d) = 0.900; R(30 d ∣ S = 7 d) = 0.677; R(30 d ∣ S = 30 d) = 0.900; R(7 d ∣ S = 1 d) = 0.562. Two independent witnesses each wrong with probability p: P(both wrong) = p², e.g. 0.01 at p = 0.1; the Bayes factor of each agreeing witness is (1−p)/p (9 at p = 0.1) and they multiply only under independence (81 at p = 0.1). Cepeda-style optimal gaps: ≈ 0.7–1.4 d for a 7-day retention interval; ≈ 3–6 d for 30 days. 604 mushaf pages at one page per day take ≈ 1.7 years, at half a page ≈ 3.3 years (session computation; the mushaf page count is the standard Madani layout and was not separately sourced).
- Tajdid's gate (paired e-value reaching 1/α_k with α_k = 0.05·2^(−k), anytime-valid by Ville's inequality) and Majlis's Thompson sampling over Beta posteriors per agent and intent are MIZAN's existing mechanisms ([CLAUDE.md](/CLAUDE.md)).
- D9 in the earlier report proposed LoRA adapters on open-weight models with a McNemar promotion test and noted that training costs "tens of dollars" on rented compute ([28 Oct 2026 report, D9](/research/reports/MIZAN%20self%20learning%20architecture.md)); the owner's constraint (2 vCPU, 2 GB, no rented GPUs) removes that option.

### Inferences

Formulas, each with its Quranic frame (all ISHARA unless noted) and the module it extends:

1. Graded belief (39:9; 58:11; 102:5–7). Each learned claim c carries Beta(a_c, b_c) over "holds when checked"; Isnad grade = sahih if the claim was established by MIZAN or by lookup in a pinned source; hasan if P(θ_c > ½) ≥ 0.95 from independent checks; da'if if only the model's words; mawdu' if contradicted by a check. Grade is a function of channel and checks, never of wording confidence (17:36). Extends Isnad.
2. Independent witnesses (2:282; Bukhari 6245, 4784). Accept a fact when Σ_i w_i ≥ τ(harm), w_i = log((1−p_i)/p_i), p_i the site's or program's learned error rate (Beta mean), and the witnesses are independent by construction (Tathabbut: second program written without seeing the first; Ahl al-Dhikr: different sites, different days). A single witness passes alone only when w_i ≥ τ, the Khuzaimah case. Extends `witnesses`/`p_true` in Ahl al-Dhikr and `tathabbut.py`.
3. Curriculum with a mastery gate (17:106; Bukhari 4993, 7372; Ahmad 23482). Seeds and Ta'lim rows are admitted in batches of about ten per intent or family; a batch is "known" when the LOO/bootstrap gate of Ta'lim passes (knowledge) and "practised" when its intents have been used in verified answers n ≥ n_min times without correction (action); the next batch opens only then. Order batches by prerequisite and by harm (low-risk, frequent intents first). Extends `talim.py`.
4. Three passes (Ibn Khaldun §36). For Lisan's instructions and for Lughah seeds: pass 1 = principal cases (the modal phrasing per intent), pass 2 = differences (near-miss pairs, code-switch variants), pass 3 = full inventory; promotion between passes by the same gate as 3. Extends Tajdid's instruction evolution.
5. Forgetting as policy (87:6–7; 18:24; 2:286; 13:17; Bukhari 5054; Abu Dawud 1394). Canonical stores have no decay (lookup). Learned items follow FSRS-6: review when R(t,S) falls to r_target; a lapse is "Again" (stability reset, no penalty elsewhere); items re-attach on a later cue (late recall still updates S). Utility weight: archive when E[benefit]·R(t) < c_store, with E[benefit] the posterior mean of a usefulness Beta from verified uses (al-Qurtubi on 39:9: unused knowledge is as if not known). Review-cycle priors: a learned item is "settled" only when S ≥ 7 d; "stable" when S ≥ 30 d; and no item is re-verified more often than every 3 days unless it lapsed (comprehension floor, by analogy only). Extends `memory/tadhkir.py`, D4/D5.
6. Monotone replacement (2:106). A learned wording, program template or instruction is replaced only when the paired e-value of (challenger beats incumbent) reaches 1/α_k, with the margin m(n_old) rising in the incumbent's verified uses (the EWC analogue in the earlier report). Extends Tajdid/D7.
7. Append-only record with a revisable layer (18:49; 6:59; 13:39). Receipts chain by h_i = SHA-256(h_{i−1} ‖ RFC 8785(e_i)); the learned layer supersedes by setting valid_to rather than deleting; Mahw's erasure is itself a receipt. Existing Sakk and Mahw; the inference is only that Nawm's consolidation (D5) writes supersessions, never rewrites.
8. Bounded capacity (2:286). Inject at most ≈ 4 consolidated chunks (Cowan) at the start of the prompt; the recall gate's threshold P > C_false/(U_hit + C_false) from D4 stands.
9. Ask the knowledgeable, then stop (16:43; 12:76; Bukhari 125). Ask source k when E[loss ∣ answer now] − E[loss ∣ ask k] > c_k, with sources ordered by the Wazn ladder; when the ladder is exhausted, emit abstention of type (a)/(b)/(c) from KQ3. Extends Ahl al-Dhikr v2 (D3) and Wazn.
10. Not stung twice (Bukhari 6133). A failure whose outcome was harmful (Hudud violation, Amanah-directed action, mawdu' shown to a user) writes a deterministic guard keyed by its signature, with no probabilistic relaxation; harmless failures write Tajriba lessons weighted by evidence. Extends `tajriba.py`, `hudud.py`, `amanah.py`.
11. Self-audit and quick repair (7:201; 59:18; 4:17). Muhasaba's daily pass stays; a correction is posted within 24 h of detection; a Tajriba lesson is kept only if the paired e-value shows the post-lesson failure rate fell (2:44; 61:2–3: a lesson not acted on is not a lesson). Extends `muhasaba.py`, `tajriba.py`.
12. Annual review with the teacher (Bukhari 6, 3624). Every kept program and wording is re-verified at least once per 365 days independent of use, and the full pass is doubled (run twice) before a production release. Extends Hifz and the deploy script.
13. Abstention as a scored output (17:36; 31:34; 12:76). Answer iff P(correct) ≥ 1 − c_abstain/c_error; Imtihan scores "Allah knows best" as correct on items labelled unknowable, and reports d′, ECE and Brier for the gate (D10).
14. Teacher once (2:33; Bukhari 67; 16:43), replacing D9. When MIZAN does not know, it asks the base model once; the answer is verified (Tathabbut, Sidq, Ahl al-Dhikr), re-expressed in MIZAN's own symbols (Bayan root-space where applicable) and kept (Hifz program, Ta'allum wording, Daftar fact, Qasd learning); the next time, MIZAN answers itself. The measured quantity is the local share S(t) = Σ_f w_f·1[P(s_f(t) ≥ τ_f) ≥ 0.95] from D9, now achieved without adapters: s_f is the per-family rate at which Hifz/Ta'allum answered without a model call and passed verification. Any Ruh/Bayan training on CPU is incremental and only on MIZAN's own verified outputs and open-licence corpora, never on the Quran or hadith as targets (87:6–7: canonical text is quoted, not learned). The cost model becomes c_total(t) = Σ_requests [1 − local(t)]·c_model, which falls as S(t) rises; this is MIZAN's inference and must be measured, not assumed.

### Gaps

- The FSRS-6 default decay parameter (w20) and the exact form of its stability update were not re-sourced in this session; numbers above use the FSRS-4.5 closed form for illustration, and the earlier report's FSRS citation should be used for implementation.
- No source was found that derives a review schedule from the hadith on 3/7/30-day recitation; the mapping of those bounds onto stability targets is this researcher's analogy.
- The independence assumption behind the p² rule is rarely true for web sources (copied text); the earlier report's "collapse copied sources" rule must be applied before counting witnesses, and the inference above does not quantify residual dependence.
- Whether CPU-only incremental training of Ruh/Bayan on verified outputs is feasible within 2 vCPU / 2 GB (tokens per second, memory) is for the ML researcher; this note only supplies the Quranic frame for keeping the base model as a teacher that teaches once.
