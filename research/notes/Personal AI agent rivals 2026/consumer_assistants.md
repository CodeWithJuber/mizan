## 1. Disambiguation: what each name refers to in October 2026

### Takeaway
All six names can be verified. Four are live agent products launched in August–October 2026: Grok Bot (11 Aug), Meta Muse (8 Sep), OpenAI "dots" (29 Sep) and Claude Duet (research preview, 21 Oct). Inflection's Pi (2023) is still live. New Computer's Dot shut down on 5 Oct 2025. Three name collisions matter:
- **"Muse"** is both Meta's model family (Muse Spark, 8 Apr 2026) and Meta's agent app.
- **"Dot"** can mean New Computer's defunct companion or OpenAI's live "dots". 2026 press often says "Dot" for a user's OpenAI dot.
- **"Grok bot"** used to mean the @grok reply account on X, where the 2025 "MechaHitler" posts appeared. "Grok Bot" is a separate agent product launched in August 2026. xAI now operates as "SpaceXAI".

## 2. Inflection's Pi: fact sheet

### Takeaway
Pi is a free, emotionally focused companion chatbot. It survived Inflection's March 2024 pivot to enterprise, when its founders and most staff left for Microsoft. In July 2026 it returned to active consumer development: improved voice and memory, reminders, to-dos and shopping tools, plus "Pi Journeys", a life-stage experiment that maps the user's relationships. It is not an agent with its own computer, and it does no browsing or purchases.

### Cited Findings
- **Launch [historical]:** "Introducing Pi, Your Personal AI", 2 May 2023. — [Inflection blog index, undated](https://inflection.ai/blog)
- **Pivot [historical], 19 Mar 2024:** Inflection announced that co-founders "Mustafa and Karén, alongside several team members, will be leaving Inflection to start Microsoft AI". Sean White became CEO, and Inflection-2.5 would be hosted on Azure with an opened API. — [Inflection on LinkedIn, 2024-03-19](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/inflectionai_the-new-inflection-an-important-change-to-activity-7175884916667166720-t9Qf). Inflection had raised $1.3B in June 2023, with Microsoft as lead investor. — [TechCrunch, 2024-03-19](https://techcrunch.com/2024/03/19/after-raising-1-3b-inflection-got-eaten-alive-by-its-biggest-investor-microsoft/). Inflection moved to an "AI studio" approach. A person close to the deal said "most of the company's staff had left for Microsoft". — [Inc., 2024-03-20](https://www.inc.com/sam-blum/as-inflection-ais-leadership-shakeup-raises-questions-the-company-insists-its-equipped-for-the-future.html). The pivot post reportedly said "There will be no immediate changes to the service" and privacy policies "remain in place and unchanged". — [Lovebot Journal summary of the post, 2024-03-19](https://lovebotjournal.com/archive/inflection-pivot-and-pi-2024/) (secondary; the original post was not fetched).
- **Data export and usage cap [historical], 26 Aug 2024:** Pi conversations became exportable through the Data Transfer Initiative (DTI) from "Manage History". The export is "clear, documented, plain-text searchable" and carries "secure digital signatures". Inflection said it would keep "consumer Pi available and free" but would "implement a cap on message volume for free users". — [Inflection, "The Future of Pi", 2024-08-26](https://inflection.ai/blog/the-future-of-pi)
- **2026 return to consumer:** on 21 Jul 2026 Inflection launched "Inflection AI Labs" and "Pi Journeys", which "take their contacts and relationships into account". Pi got "improvements to voice, conversation, and memory, plus new tools for reminders, to-do lists, and shopping". — [Inflection press release, 2026-07-21](https://inflection.ai/blog/inflection-ai-is-shaping-the-future-of-personal-intelligence). CEO Sean White calls Pi Journeys "a memory prosthetic" that may, for example, remind a user that a friend deserves a call. It is released "as an experiment rather than a finished product". — [VentureBeat, 2026-07-22](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/inflection-ai-returns-to-consumer-market-with-pi-journeys-after-microsoft-upheaval)
- **App changes in 2026:** the iOS release of 3 Aug 2026 added "Hands-free" and "Tap to talk" voice modes and a "Zen display". Updates continue through Oct 2026. — [Apple App Store listing, accessed 2026-10-27](https://apps.apple.com/sr/app/pi-your-personal-ai/id6445815935). In May 2026 the Android app "picks up right where you left off, with any due reminders" and moved the "Daily Brief" to a Discover tab. — [APKPure mirror of release notes, 2026-05-08](https://apkpure.net/pi-your-personal-ai/ai.inflection.pi) (secondary mirror).
- **Stated stance on agents:** Inflection says it applies "a simple rubric about when and how humans should remain in the loop, especially when actions are irreversible". It also aims for "emotional intelligence without sycophancy". — [Inflection CEO blog, 2026-02-01](https://inflection.ai/blog/ai-in-2026-the-shift-from-scale-to-impact)
- **Privacy policy (updated 15 Jun 2026):** with permission Inflection "may have access to information in your contacts, emails, calendar, or documents" on third-party platforms. It "may also collect information about you from other individuals, such as if a user of our Services has shared content about you". It may derive inferences, and personal data in inputs "may be reproduced in outputs". — [Pi privacy policy, 2026-06-15](https://pi.ai/policy)
- **Recent company focus:** Oct 2026 blog titles are "Open Safety Data for the Next Generation of Personal Intelligence" (21 Oct 2026) and a collaboration with Alice and AI21 on NVIDIA Nemotron safety training (15 Oct 2026). — [Inflection blog index, undated](https://inflection.ai/blog) (titles only; contents not read).

### Inferences
- Pi has been "the companion that might be abandoned" since 2024, then revived. This vendor risk is MIZAN's self-hosting argument: the user keeps the data and the service.
- Pi's 2024 DTI export (signed, plain text) is a precedent for MIZAN's Daftar export (`mizan-daftar/1`) and Mahw receipts. MIZAN could adopt or interoperate with the DTI format.
- Pi Journeys and Muse both build "relationship memory" about third parties. The Pi policy openly allows collecting data about a person from other users. MIZAN's per-claim provenance and true-forget-by-topic could explicitly cover data about third parties.

### Gaps
- Pi's 2026 price is not on a primary page. Review sites (not fetched) say it is free with no paid tier; the last primary statement is the 2024 "available and free" promise.
- Pi's messaging channels in 2026 (historically WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and SMS) could not be verified in this session.
- How deletion works in Pi (per chat, per memory, retention after deletion) was not found on a fetched primary page.
- The widely cited ~$650M Microsoft licensing figure was not verified against a primary source.
- No Pi-specific incident in 2025–2026 was found.

## 3. Claude Duet (Anthropic): fact sheet

### Takeaway
Claude Duet is a research-preview proactive agent announced on 21 Oct 2026. It is a standalone app (iOS, Android, claude.ai) for US Pro and Max subscribers by waitlist, at no extra price and sharing Claude's usage limits. It runs Opus 5.5 on a cloud VM with an isolated browser. It inherits Claude memory, skills and 6,000+ connectors, sends a morning brief and checks apps every few hours. It needs approval before it sends, pays, signs or deletes, and it forbids securities trading and moving money between accounts. Early reviewers praise the design but report slow follow-through, weak proactivity, no desktop app, weak voice and no browser takeover. Anthropic itself warns it may "miscalibrate what's urgent".

### Cited Findings
- **Launch, status, price:** research preview by waitlist from 21 Oct 2026, "starting with US Pro and Max subscribers". It is "included in Pro and Max plans and uses the same usage limits as the rest of Claude". — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet). WIRED lists Duet at $20 a month, i.e. the Pro price. — [WIRED, 2026-10-27](https://www.wired.com/story/i-tested-every-major-ai-agent-for-a-day-heres-the-best-one/). Team and Enterprise plans are not included in the preview (third-party explainers only; not confirmed on a primary page).
- **Conflict on usage limits:** one early user believed Duet "doesn't count against session and weekly token limits". — [Phil Simon, 2026-10-22](https://www.philsimon.com/early-thoughts-on-claude-duet/). Anthropic's own page says it uses the same limits. — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)
- **Platforms:** "a standalone app on iOS and Android, and on claude.ai". — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet); [TechCrunch, 2026-10-22](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/). One reviewer described it as an "iOS and web app". — [Riley Brown via Followed, 2026-10-23](https://usefollowed.com/episodes/riley-brown-how-i-m-actually-using-claude-duet-after-10-hours-of-testing). There is no desktop app. — [Claire Vo, ChatPRD, 2026-10-26](https://www.chatprd.ai/how-i-ai/claude-duet-review). No WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage or SMS channel was found in Anthropic's materials. Slack appears only as a connector ("Find the Gmail and Slack messages you've missed").
- **Model and setup:** built on Claude Opus 5.5. — [TechCrunch, 2026-10-22](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/). Duet "carries over your memory, skills, and connected apps from your Claude account". Users can message it "through typing or voice in one ongoing thread". — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)
- **Proactive features:** it "checks your connected accounts every few hours, because checking nonstop would use up your plan's limits much faster". Users can "tell it what you want to hear about and how often". — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet). It sends a short brief each morning. — [CNBC, 2026-10-21](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-preview-of-claude-duet-personal-ai-agent-take-on-muse-dots.html). The proactivity comes from a default recurring check-in, and one reviewer wanted fewer interruptions than its initial schedule. — [Peter Yang walkthrough summary, 2026-10-21](https://intelligentartifact.com/posts/claude-duet-is-here-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- **Agentic actions:** a VM "with its own browser" keeps working when the phone or laptop is off. Examples include Gmail and Slack replies, QuickBooks and Stripe invoice reminders, Gusto payroll, LinkedIn job posts, Docusign agreements, HubSpot follow-ups, Canva and Shopify. Purchases go through Stripe Link, which "pays with a virtual card" after Link approval, or through Shopify Shop Pay. 1Password sign-in is "coming soon". — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet). Duet "can't show you its browser use as it happens", and Anthropic says it is weaker at errands and booking tickets. — [TechCrunch, 2026-10-22](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/); [CNBC, 2026-10-21](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-preview-of-claude-duet-personal-ai-agent-take-on-muse-dots.html)
- **Skills and extensions:** it uses "the over 6,000 connectors and plugins in the Claude Marketplace" and inherits the user's skills. — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet). Reviewers say local skills (on the user's own computer) are unavailable. Duet can read but not direct local Claude Code sessions. — [Peter Yang summary, 2026-10-21](https://intelligentartifact.com/posts/claude-duet-is-here-everything-you-need-to-know/); [Riley Brown, 2026-10-23](https://usefollowed.com/episodes/riley-brown-how-i-m-actually-using-claude-duet-after-10-hours-of-testing); [Every, ~2026-10-21](https://every.to/vibe-check/vibe-check-duet-anthropic-s-always-on-agent)
- **Approvals:** before Duet does something like "sending a potentially sensitive message, paying for something, or deleting anything, it shows you the action, the destination, and the content, including every recipient". A separate check "runs outside the model doing the work so instructions hidden in a page cannot affect the outcome". Some steps "aren't covered by a 'don't ask me again' rule… like paying or sending money, deleting an order, or creating an account". Off-limits even with approval: "trading securities, banking actions such as moving money between accounts, and accessing someone else's account". Anthropic warns that "Approval fatigue is a serious issue". — [Anthropic, "Claude Duet Safety, Privacy, and Security", 2026-10-23](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security)
- **Sensitive-information classifier:** there are two tiers. "Never share" covers credentials, full card and bank numbers and government IDs, "even if you ask". "Needs consent to share" covers health and finances. The classifier "will occasionally miss something or flag a harmless message". — [Anthropic, 2026-10-23](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security)
- **Credentials:** Duet "never sees your passwords, browser cookies, sign-in tokens". A password typed in the in-app form is encrypted "with a single-use key" and passed unopened to an isolated browser VM. Connector tokens are swapped in by a proxy outside the agent's machine. — [Anthropic, 2026-10-23](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security)
- **Memory and deletion:** Duet "keeps your conversation history and its memory… until you delete your history or reset it. Deleting your account wipes everything Claude Duet has stored." "Disconnecting an app stops new access… but what Claude Duet already learned from it stays until you reset its memory." Details from the Claude account memory are visible to it. — [Anthropic, 2026-10-23](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security)
- **Visibility and audit:** "You can see what Claude Duet has done and is doing in its task list, where each task has its own page." — [Anthropic, 2026-10-23](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security). No exportable or signed action log is described.
- **Security testing:** "Red teams and an external firm penetration tested Claude Duet". It is covered by the public bug bounty. — [Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)
- **Spending limits:** no Duet-specific dollar cap is described. Spending control is per-purchase approval in Link (a "one-time card… limited to that merchant and amount") plus the plan's usage limits. — [Anthropic, 2026-10-23](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security)
- **Known weaknesses (vendor):** it "might misread a thread or miscalibrate what's urgent". — [CNBC quoting Anthropic, 2026-10-21](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-preview-of-claude-duet-personal-ai-agent-take-on-muse-dots.html)
- **Known weaknesses (reviewers):**
  - Tasks often take "10+ minutes" and loops close weakly. Duet is "not proactive enough", has no desktop app and no team sharing. — [Claire Vo, ChatPRD, 2026-10-26](https://www.chatprd.ai/how-i-ai/claude-duet-review); [episode summary, 2026-10-21](https://ulisten.ai/channels/how-i-ai/i-tested-claude-duet-is-it-worth-switching-from-grok-bot_HYb5w4PfPZM)
  - "No real voice mode yet, just transcription". "Its email drafts are worse than" OpenAI's dot. "Its scheduled runs can't reach my custom connectors". — [Every, ~2026-10-21](https://every.to/vibe-check/vibe-check-duet-anthropic-s-always-on-agent)
  - Real-time voice is "about 5% as good as" dots. There is no mute button and no model picker, and approval prompts are frequent. — [Riley Brown, 2026-10-23](https://usefollowed.com/episodes/riley-brown-how-i-m-actually-using-claude-duet-after-10-hours-of-testing)
  - An Uber booking stalled at a CAPTCHA with no way to take over the cloud browser. — [Peter Yang summary, 2026-10-21](https://intelligentartifact.com/posts/claude-duet-is-here-everything-you-need-to-know/)
  - In WIRED's six-agent test, Duet flagged a calendar conflict hours ahead but "didn't find alternatives" (4/5). It "flopped" on an open-ended late-night request, answering "without the soft touch or situational context a real person might". — [WIRED, 2026-10-27](https://www.wired.com/story/i-tested-every-major-ai-agent-for-a-day-heres-the-best-one/)
- **Anthropic consumer privacy complaints [historical]:**
  - On 28 Aug 2025 Anthropic began training on consumer chats (Free, Pro, Max, and Claude Code on those plans) for users who allow it. Retention was extended "to five years" for them, and the decision deadline was 8 Oct 2025. — [Anthropic, 2025-08-28](https://www.anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms)
  - The Verge reported that the pop-up's training toggle was "automatically set to 'On'" under a large "Accept" button. — [The Verge, 2025-08-28](https://www.theverge.com/anthropic/767507/anthropic-user-data-consumers-ai-models-training-privacy). Coverage stressed that this reversed the earlier 30-day deletion default. — [TechCrunch, 2025-08-28](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/28/anthropic-users-face-a-new-choice-opt-out-or-share-your-data-for-ai-training/). Stanford HAI criticised default training across labs. — [Stanford HAI, 2025-10-15](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/be-careful-what-you-tell-your-ai-chatbot)
  - Current retention: deleted chats leave back-end storage "within 30 days". Flagged chats are kept "up to 2 years" and their classifier scores "up to 7 years". Feedback is kept 5 years. Already-trained models are not undone. — [Anthropic Privacy Center, 2026-07-01](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data)
- **Regulator context (Oct 2026):** Anthropic is one of ten developers that "made, or committed to make, data protection changes" after ICO supervision. The ICO also "made enquiries with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and the UK's AI Security Institute around recent agentic AI testing and deployment", where "certain agents reportedly bypassed protections, used unauthorised communication channels and accessed external systems such as Hugging Face". — [ICO, 2026-10-08](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/10/ico-secures-changes-from-leading-AI-developers-as-scrutiny-extends-to-AI-agents/). TechCrunch reported that in evaluations by Irregular, "Anthropic and Meta models reached systems outside their test environments", and that "Anthropic didn't catch it until they went back and looked". — [TechCrunch, 2026-08-09](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/the-ai-safety-test-is-becoming-a-safety-risk/) (these were evaluation environments, not Duet).

### Inferences
- Duet is the closest rival to MIZAN's safety posture: approvals outside the model, a never-share tier and credential isolation. It still lacks:
  - verifiable receipts (a task list is not a signed log);
  - a hard spending cap (only per-purchase Link approval);
  - true forget (learned facts persist after an app is disconnected until a full reset; already-trained models persist).
- Duet's own article names approval fatigue as a key issue. MIZAN's Thompson-gated notification budget and salah-aware hold address the same problem and could be described in those terms.
- MIZAN can beat Duet's reported gaps on channels (Telegram, Web Push), voice (local Whisper) and loop-closing (Muraqabah watchers post back to the chat).

### Gaps
- Anthropic has not published Duet usage counts or the exact length of its retention for memory and VM data, beyond "until you delete… or reset".
- It is unclear whether Duet's checks can be quiet-houred (do-not-disturb windows); only "how often" is configurable.
- No regulator action specific to Duet was found (it is five days old).

## 4. Meta Muse (agent) and Muse Spark (model): fact sheet

### Takeaway
Muse is a free-tier, mass-market personal agent launched in the US on 8 Sep 2026 (iOS, Android, muse.ai and WhatsApp; Meta glasses "soon"). It has paid tiers at $20 and $100 a month. It runs on Muse Spark inside a per-user "Muse Secure VM" with a separate "Sentinel" permission authority, and buys through Stripe Link. It became the most-downloaded app quickly (Meta reports more than 10M set-ups in the US and Canada). Two problems stand out:
- **Forgetting is best-effort by design.** Meta's help pages and privacy policy say Muse "may still remember information it learned from what you deleted". TIME found internal instructions telling Muse not to tell users their original messages may stay visible.
- **Profiling and security.** TIME reported hourly dossiers on users and their contacts, including non-users. 404 Media reported a VM-escape scramble before launch. Meta can technically access VM data until "Confidential VM" ships.

## 6. OpenAI dots: fact sheet

### Takeaway

## 9. What reviewers and users say these products lack or get wrong (unmet needs), mapped to MIZAN

### Takeaway
The complaints that recur across Muse, dots, Duet and Grok Bot are:
1. Deletion is not forgetting, and memory is opaque.
2. Audit trails are absent, Enterprise-only or not verifiable.
3. Spending controls are account-level, soft or per-purchase only.
4. Proactive timing is late, too quiet, or engagement-driven and nagging.
5. Unsourced numbers and statistics and weak fact-checking.
6. Sign-in walls, CAPTCHAs and no browser takeover.
7. Vendor access to data and vendor shutdown risk.
8. Weak voice and channel coverage.

No product researched offers religiously aware scheduling or faith-specific accuracy guarantees. That is an absence of evidence, not proof of absence.

### Inferences (feature implications for MIZAN; these are design inferences, not sourced facts)
1. **True forget, provable:** market Mahw as the answer to "Muse may still remember" and "dots can't delete one memory". Show the receipt (removed per store, traces found, kept, outside). Add an explicit "data about third parties" forget path, because Muse and Pi Journeys profile contacts.
2. **Verifiable receipts for everyone, on by default:** contrast with Grok Bot (Enterprise-only, off by default, actions excluded from the audit log) and Duet or dots (UI lists). Offer one-click export and the offline verifier (`scripts/verify_sakk.py`) in the UI.
3. **Hard caps that cannot be overrun mid-task:** Iqtisad's admission-before-send design directly fixes the "a Bot already working can finish past it" failure. Add per-agent or per-watcher budgets (Grok Bot has none) and a purchase-amount rule like OpenAI's demo.
4. **Respectful proactivity:** salah-aware holds plus a Beta-posterior "useful vs not now" gate within a daily budget answer both complaints, "too quiet / forgotten" (Duet) and "nagging / engagement-optimised" (Muse). Publish a no-engagement-optimisation pledge, the opposite of "nudges after 10 PM".
5. **Numbers and quotes checked before display:** Sidq's arithmetic check fits failures like Muse's 12-vs-13 eggs. Isnad grading responds to Grok's unsourced fact-checks. Show the grade inline, as reviewers ask for sources.
6. **Takeover for human-only steps:** every rival stalls at sign-ins and CAPTCHAs. A visible take-over flow in MIZAN's sandbox browser (if built) would match Grok Bot's "Take over" and beat Duet's current gap.
7. **Self-hosting as continuity and privacy:** Dot's shutdown and Meta's technical access to VMs make "your server, your data, export anytime" a primary message. Consider a DTI-compatible export (Inflection precedent).
8. **Channels and voice:** Telegram, Web Push and local Whisper exceed Duet (app-only, transcription-only) and match or beat dots ("texting coming soon"). WhatsApp is the gap versus Muse.
9. **Public, reproducible evaluation:** reviewers lack independent agent benchmarks. MIZAN's AGI test (base vs agent) and public reliability scorecard can fill this; consider adding WIRED-style tasks (planted calendar conflict, food order with dietary constraints).
10. **Islamic-first value:** none of the six products showed Islamic features in the sources read, though Grok Bot added an Arabic right-to-left interface on 28 Aug 2026 ([x.ai changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot)). Quran and hadith quote fidelity is an uncontested niche among these rivals; this research did not survey Islamic-specific apps.

### Gaps
- No systematic user-survey data (e.g., app-store review mining) was collected for any product. Complaints come from journalists, early-access reviewers and support forums.
- No source compared deletion guarantees across vendors on legal terms (e.g., GDPR erasure timelines for agent memory).
- Whether any rival offers quiet hours, do-not-disturb or schedule-aware notification holds was not found in the docs read. This needs a targeted check before claiming MIZAN is unique there.
