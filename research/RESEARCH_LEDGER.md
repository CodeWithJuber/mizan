## Quranic motivation and epistemic limits

Quran 17:36, 30:22, 14:4 and 2:31 were discussed in the user's original research as guidance about knowledge, languages and names. Their presence in the research does not establish a fixed operator for each Arabic letter, a universal translation formula or a consciousness mechanism. Scriptural text, tafsir, lexicographic interpretation and experimental model hypotheses remain separately attributed.

This release does not invent a theological proof to repair an inconsistent metric. It retains the user's roots hypothesis as an experimentally testable direction and specifies the data, controls and open questions in `MORE_DATA_NEEDED.md`.
